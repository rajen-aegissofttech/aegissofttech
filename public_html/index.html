Customer Support Automation Solution for a Healthcare Tech Company

A healthcare tech company was experiencing challenges related to slow customer support because of growing ticket volume and insufficient staff to triage them. Aegis developed a layer of conversational AI that understood the customers’ intents, resolved routine queries automatically, and assigned complex ones to an agent. A full-fledged solution delivered in 8 weeks helped resolve the issue.

Enterprise Data Modernization for a Financial Services Company

A financial services company had data spread across 5 separate legacy systems, making reporting a tedious manual process that required significant effort. Aegis migrated all systems to a Snowflake data warehouse and added Power BI dashboards on top of that. As a result, the reporting process took minutes while previously it would have taken days.

Read full case study

Cloud Migration for a Retail & eCommerce Company

A retail company faced the challenge of limited performance due to outdated infrastructure, as traffic grew during the sales season. Aegis migrated critical applications to Azure and implemented DevOps pipelines to make deployments easier. As a result, the performance was stable during high traffic, infrastructure costs decreased, and the development team was able to ship updates without stress.
