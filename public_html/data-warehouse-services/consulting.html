Aegis Softtech Data Warehouse Consulting Services

Inconsistent reporting, cumbersome data pipelines, increasing expenses, or confusion over technology choices? Our data warehousing consultants identify underlying issues, evaluate your options, and choose the best way forward before you commit to major changes.

Data Warehouse Assessment and Strategy

We assess your data environment, reporting needs, workloads, and business objectives to identify areas that need improvement and technical limitations.

Our consultants work with business stakeholders and technical teams to define requirements, establish measurable objectives, and develop an appropriate data warehouse strategy aligned with your business goals.

Architecture and Cloud Data Warehouse Consulting

Our cloud data warehouse consulting services include the evaluation of platforms such as Snowflake, Amazon Redshift, Google BigQuery, and Azure Synapse Analytics.

We compare architectural options, assess cloud readiness, and identify technical limitations to recommend the best architecture depending on your workloads, scalability needs, security requirements, and budget.

ETL/ELT and Data Quality Consulting

We assess pipeline architecture, transformation dependencies, and data quality processes to identify issues that affect reliability, scalability, and maintainability.

Our consultants recommend improvements in modular design, data profiling, validation, testing, and handling of schema changes that can help you solve underlying problems before making extensive pipeline changes.

Data Governance and Security Consulting

We evaluate your existing governance practices, data ownership, access requirements, and quality controls to identify gaps based on your evolving data environment.

Our consultants recommend the right governance frameworks, monitoring practices, access controls, and policy improvements aligned with your organization's data management and security requirements.

Data Warehouse Performance and Cost Assessment

We examine workload patterns, resource usage, and data processing requirements to identify performance bottlenecks and potential cost inefficiencies.

Our consultants provide recommendations according to technical findings, assisting your team in making architectural trade-offs, prioritizing changes, and making informed investment decisions.

Our Data Warehouse Consulting Approach: From Assessment to an Actionable Roadmap

Our consulting process is designed to move from understanding your requirements to setting the next steps.

01

Discovery and Scoping

We discuss your objectives, challenges, and priorities with key stakeholders to establish the scope of the engagement.

Outcome: Defined objectives and scope.

02

Technical Evaluation

We review the relevant systems, documentation, and requirements, examine available options, and discuss their implications with your team.

Outcome: Findings and evaluated options.

03

Recommendations and Roadmap

We present our recommendations, explain key trade-offs, and outline priorities and next steps for your team.

Outcome: A prioritized roadmap with defined next steps.
