Our Cloud Data Warehousing Services

Every managed cloud data warehouse service we offer is built to deliver measurable business impact for you—faster insights, reduced costs, and scalable architectures.

Here is a glimpse into what we deliver and how it impacts your business growth:

What We Deliver

We design a sustainable data warehouse architecture centered around ingestion patterns, consumption models, storage layers, governance frameworks, and cost-performance trade-offs.

Impact:

You enjoy a clear blueprint that reduces migration risk, ensures warehouse scalability, accelerates implementation, and aligns with business objectives.

What We Deliver

We provision your warehouse, set up ingestion pipelines, configure secure access layers, and integrate ETL/ELT frameworks. Our implementation adheres to automated infrastructure-as-code and cloud-native best practices, ensuring predictable and repeatable deployments.

Impact:

You receive a robust, production-ready data environment with minimal manual intervention and seamless data flow.

What We Deliver

We offer data migration services from legacy systems, such as on-premise warehouses, CRMs, data marts, or ERPs, into a unified cloud warehouse. Our zero-downtime approach ensures your business teams continue working without disruption.

Impact:

You have a consolidated and highly reliable data foundation that significantly improves reporting consistency by eliminating silos.

What We Deliver

We analyze workloads, set up auto-scaling, automate clustering and partitioning, optimize queries, and build FinOps dashboards for ongoing cost visibility and transparency. Our team fine-tunes compute resources to minimize waste without compromising performance.

Impact:

Your business benefits from faster query execution, higher concurrency, predictable cloud spending, and reduced monthly compute/storage costs.

What We Deliver

We utilize tools, such as Kafka, Pub/Sub, Azure Event Hub, or Kinesis for streaming ingestion. We also implement lakehouse patterns, real-time transformation layers, and AI/ML-ready pipelines to ensure you can access fresh, high-quality data wherever needed.

Impact:

You benefit from real-time dashboards, automated insights, advanced forecasting, and a competitive advantage powered by modern analytics.

Our Engagement Models

Fixed-Price (Scope-Limited) Model

Ideal for short-term projects with clear requirements or repeatable implementations. We agree on a well-defined timeline and deliverables upfront, fixing the price accordingly.

Time & Materials (T&M) Model

Ideal for complex, evolving, or exploratory projects where requirements are dynamic. The client only pays for the actual effort spent, which is calculated hourly/daily/monthly rates for consultants, engineers, and architects.

Outcome-Based (SLA-Tied) Model

Ideal for long-term partnerships where measurable outcomes are critical. It’s also suitable for clients who prefer to pay for results. The final pricing is linked to achieving stated business outcomes.

Proof of Concept (PoC) Pricing

It’s suitable for those seeking to validate ROI or technical compatibility before committing. It is preferred for a small-scale pilot engagement to validate feasibility before a full rollout.
