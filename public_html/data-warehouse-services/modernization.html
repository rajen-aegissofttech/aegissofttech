Data Warehouse Modernization Services We Offer

Modernizing a warehouse encompasses a multi-layer transformation. Our services ensure every layer is future-ready and aligned with your business goals.

We modernize legacy platforms, such as Oracle, Teradata, IBM DB2, Netezza, SQL Server, and traditional ETL systems. Our data warehouse migration services transform them to cloud-native data architectures with minimal risk.

We create a blueprint for your future-ready warehouse with target platform selection, cost modeling, architecture design, and workload mapping.

Impact:

  • Faster time-to-value
  • Architectural clarity

FAQs

Data warehouse modernization is the process of upgrading on-premises, siloed, or outdated data warehouses into modern, cloud-ready platforms. We help you modernize your DWH for advanced analytics and real-time workloads.

Every modernization program differs depending on complexity, integrations, and data volumes, and thus completes in varying timelines. Most mid-sized programs, however, take between 8 and 16 weeks.

We help mitigate downtime, migration inconsistencies, data loss, BI report breakage, performance degradation, and schema mismatches, among others.

The best cloud depends on your workloads. The top ones are Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery, Databricks, and Synapse. We evaluate the right one for you according to cost, governance, business needs, and scale.
