Five Ways We Scope a Business Central Engagement

Whether you're replacing a legacy ERP, moving off Dynamics NAV, or standing up finance and operations for the first time, our Business Central implementation services are scoped around your business, not a fixed package.

End-to-end delivery: discovery, solution design, configuration, data migration, integration, testing, training, and go-live support. This is our default engagement for SMB, mid-market, and multi-entity organizations that need one accountable partner.

Discovery, process mapping, and solution design across Finance, Sales, Purchasing, Inventory, Warehouse, Manufacturing, Jobs, and Planning configured around your actual workflows, not a generic template.

AL development, extensions, APIs, and integrations for the requirements that genuinely can't be met through configuration built on the extension model so customizations survive the next upgrade.

For companies replacing an aging or unsupported ERP, NAV, SAP Business One, an old Sage system, or a spreadsheet and QuickBooks setup we run a structured cutover that protects historical data and daily operations during the switch.

Our Business Central Implementation Methodology

This is the actual sequence we run on a Business Central implementation, not a marketing diagram. Each phase produces a specific artifact the next phase depends on, which is why the order matters and doesn't get compressed to hit a deadline.

01

Discovery & current-state assessment

Our Business Central consulting services assess your systems and workflows to define implementation priorities and reduce unnecessary customization.

02

Requirements & process analysis

We compare your current processes to standard Business Central functionality and define the target process prioritized by business impact, not by what's easiest to build.

03

Solution design

Chart of accounts, dimensions, posting groups, number series, and approval workflows get designed here the decisions that are expensive to unwind after go-live.

04

Configuration & customization

We configure everything standard Business Central can handle first, and scope AL development or AppSource extensions only for the genuine gaps.

05

Integration & data migration

We clean, migrate, and validate your business data. Our Dynamics 365 consulting services help connect Business Central with other Dynamics 365 applications.

06

Testing & user acceptance

Scripted testing against your real processes, followed by UAT with the people who'll use the system daily not just the project sponsors.

07

Training & go-live

Role-based training and a cutover plan with a defined rollback point, so go-live day has no open questions.

08

Post-go-live support

Hypercare in the first weeks, then a transition to steady-state support the same specialists who built it, not a new support desk.

Industries Where We've Implemented Business Central Extensively

Business Central behaves differently by industry the moment you get past the chart of accounts. These are the sectors where we've configured, customized, and supported it long enough to know where the standard functionality ends.

Distribution & wholesale

Multi-warehouse inventory, landed cost, drop shipment, and vendor rebate structures. Warehouse Management configuration is usually the biggest lever here, and one of the areas we most often recommend phasing rather than deploying in full on day one.

Manufacturing

BOMs, routings, capacity planning, and subcontracting. Manufacturing is consistently one of the modules that needs the most configuration work to match a plant's actual production flow, and it's where we spend the most discovery time before committing to a design.

Professional & project services

Jobs, resource costing, and WIP tracking, usually paired with time and billing workflows that need to reconcile against Finance without manual journal entries.

Retail & multi-location

High transaction volume, POS integration, and inventory visibility across locations architecture decisions here are made with monthly transaction counts in mind from day one, not retrofitted after go-live.

Multi-entity & multi-country groups

Intercompany transactions, consolidations, and currency handling across companies. This is where chart of accounts and dimension design during solution design either pays off or creates years of reconciliation overhead.
