Our Dynamics 365 Training Delivery Methods

Organizations have varied preferences, teams, and needs when it comes to training. We provide flexible Microsoft Dynamics 365 training delivery formats, tailored to your organizational needs.

Instructor-Led Training

Our instructor-led sessions are available both onsite and virtually. The training includes live demos, business discussions, hands-on exercises, and Q&A sessions.

This format allows:

  • Live product demonstrations tied to real business scenarios
  • Open discussions around process and workflow questions
  • Practical exercises participants complete during the session
  • Questions and answers to discuss team-specific issues

Best For: Organizations where different teams use Dynamics 365 in different ways. Teams require training that focuses on their specific jobs as opposed to one-size-fits-all sessions.

Role-Based Workshops

Different users perform different tasks in Dynamics 365. Instead of providing the same training to everyone, we organize role-specific workshops for:

  • Sales teams
  • Customer service representatives
  • Finance users
  • Warehouse teams
  • Field technicians
  • Business analysts
  • Administrators
  • Developers

Best for: Organizations where different departments use Dynamics 365 in different ways and need training tailored to their specific tasks rather than a one-size-fits-all session.

Hands-On Sandbox Training

People learn faster when they use the system themselves. This hands-on training is carried out in sandbox or non-production environments. It allows the user to go through various processes in the system without putting any production data at risk. Our hands-on session includes:

  • Guided exercises in a safe, non-production environment
  • Practicing various business processes in a non-production environment
  • Room for making mistakes and learning.
  • Confidence-building before working in the live system

Best For: Groups and individuals who want to practice before going live. It is also good for people who learn from doing, not from watching.

Self-Paced Learning

Organizations with distributed or global teams often require flexible learning options. We provide self-paced learning resources including:

  • Recorded training sessions
  • Video tutorials
  • User manuals
  • Step-by-step guides
  • Frequently asked questions
  • Quick reference documents

Best For: Teams spread throughout time zones, new employees who require onboarding content at their own pace, and anyone requiring a refresher without scheduling a live training session.

Train-the-Trainer Courses

For many businesses, developing internal know-how is better than hiring external consultants for everything. The Train-the-Trainer course helps selected individuals become internal Dynamics 365 champions capable of:

  • Supporting colleagues
  • Answering day-to-day questions
  • Training new employees
  • Reinforcing best practices
  • Encouraging long-term adoption

Best for: Organizations looking to use Dynamics 365 in the long run and seeking decreased reliance on external consultants, alongside developing internal know-how.
