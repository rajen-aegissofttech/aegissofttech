Our Dynamics 365 Migration Framework

A six-stage migration path, planned before we touch your data

Every Dynamics 365 migration we run follows the same disciplined sequence — the order is what keeps your systems live while the move happens.

01

Discovery & system audit

We document your existing data sources, integrations, and workflows, as well as identify any silos, duplications, and inconsistencies before scoping the project.

02

Migration strategy & roadmap

We select the appropriate Dynamics 365 components, map their migration schedule, and plan a realistic timeline, budget, and risk management plan that takes into account your company’s calendar.

03

Data mapping & cleansing

Fields are mapped to their Dynamics 365 counterparts; duplicate records are consolidated, any format issues corrected, and old data is archived before migration.

04

Test migration & validation

A sandbox run moves a representative data set first, so we can catch mapping or integration issues before your production data ever moves.

05

Production cutover

The full migration runs in scheduled phases during low-activity windows, with reconciliation checks confirming record counts and data accuracy at each step.

06

Go-live & post-migration support

We support user onboarding, monitor the new environment, and stay on for fixes and fine-tuning as your team settles into Dynamics 365. We offer a 2 to 4 month post-go-live support period.

How We Solve Common Dynamics 365 Migration Challenges

All CRM migrations have their associated risks, ranging from poor data quality to integration issues and user acceptance issues. Our Microsoft Certified migration experts spot these risks upfront and devise mitigations in all our projects.

Migration ChallengeHow We Solve It
Data loss and inconsistent recordsOur first step before migration is an audit of your data, followed by cleansing, deduplication, and mapping to Dynamics 365 entities.
Business downtime during migrationMigration is performed in phases; test migrations take place in sandbox environments, and production migrations occur during low business activity times. Rollbacks are planned for each production migration.
Broken integrations after migrationAll the existing integrations undergo assessment, and we either rewrite or improve them using native connectors for Microsoft products, Power Platform, APIs, Azure Integration Services, or middleware. End-to-end testing will be performed to make sure that all business processes continue working uninterrupted.
Legacy customizations and workflowsOur consultants examine all the existing plugins, workflows, custom entities, business rules, and Power Automate flows to find out what needs to be migrated, improved, or retired for long-term benefit.
Poor user adoption after go-liveWe provide administrator training, user training by roles, documentation, and post-go-live support to let people switch to Dynamics 365 without any productivity disruptions.
