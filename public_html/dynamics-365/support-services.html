Our Dynamics 365 Support Services

Every organization uses Dynamics 365 differently. Our support services are designed to keep your environment secure, stable, and aligned with your changing requirements.

Technical Troubleshooting

Application errors, configuration problems, access issues, integrations, performance concerns — we work through all of it without touching what's already stable.

Help Desk Support

A user stuck on a system error loses the whole day if nobody's answering. We investigate the problem, guide the user through it, and get operations back to normal without turning a five-minute issue into a five-hour one.

Functional Configuration Support

Business processes don't stay still, so the configuration shouldn't either. We review it, refine the workflows, and adjust what's needed so the platform keeps matching how your teams actually work, not how they worked a year ago.

Bug Fixes

A temporary fix just means the same issue comes back later, usually at a worse time. We diagnose the actual cause, resolve what keeps recurring, and restore the functionality it was affecting.

Performance Optimization & Monitoring

More users, more data, more processes — performance shouldn't degrade just because the system's being used the way it was meant to be. We assess what's actually constraining it and recommend the configuration or technical changes that fix that.

Microsoft Release and Update Support

Microsoft's update cycle doesn't wait for convenient timing. We assess what's coming, validate the impact on your specific environment, support the testing and deployment, and help teams adopt what's actually useful without the disruption that usually comes with it.

Enhancements and Custom Development

Standard functionality eventually hits a wall for every business. We build the targeted enhancement, refine what's already customized, and improve automation, keeping every change aligned with the broader architecture instead of bolted onto it.

Integration and Data Flow Support

Microsoft applications, Power Platform, third-party solutions, legacy systems, external data sources — we keep the connections between all of them working, so data actually flows instead of getting stuck somewhere in between.

User Training and Adoption

A feature nobody knows how to use might as well not exist. We provide role-specific guidance, training, and knowledge sharing that build real confidence, not just a one-time walkthrough people forget by the following week.

System Assessment and Advisory

Configuration, performance, security, architecture, usage — we review all of it and hand back recommendations tied to what your business actually needs next, not a generic health-check report.

Our Dynamics 365 Support Process

Every request for support goes through a well-defined process that is in line with ITIL standards and guarantees quicker resolution and continuous improvement.

01

Support Request Received

Logged through our ticketing system, capturing the issue, business impact, and details needed to begin investigation.

02

Priority Classification

Severity, urgency, and business impact are assessed, and a response begins according to the agreed SLA.

03

Root Cause Analysis

Consultants investigate the underlying cause so we resolve the source of the problem, not just the symptom.

04

Resolution Planning

We define the resolution approach, estimate impact, identify dependencies, and prepare an action plan.

05

Testing

Every fix is validated in a controlled environment before release.

06

Deployment

The solution is deployed to production using controlled release practices.

07

Validation

We confirm the issue is fully resolved and the solution performs as intended.

08

Knowledge Documentation

The issue, root cause, and resolution are logged for faster future resolution.

09

Continuous Monitoring

We track system health to prevent repeat incidents and surface new improvements.

Flexible Dynamics 365 Support Engagement Models

Choose a support approach that fits your business needs, from on-demand assistance to dedicated teams and ongoing application maintenance. Get the right level of expertise, responsiveness, and continuity without paying for support you don’t need.

On-Demand Support

Best for businesses that need expert help from time to time without committing to a recurring support arrangement. Get assistance with technical issues, user queries, configuration changes, or smaller enhancements as they arise.

Best suited for:

  • One-off technical issues
  • User and functional queries
  • Minor configuration changes
  • Short-term enhancement needs

SLA-Based Support

A structured support model with defined response and resolution targets based on the severity of each issue. It provides predictable service, clear escalation paths, and priority handling when business-critical problems occur.

Best suited for:

  • Business-critical applications
  • Defined response time requirements
  • Priority issue resolution
  • Structured support processes

Dedicated Support Team

Get a dedicated team that develops a deeper understanding of your applications, workflows, integrations, and business requirements. This model provides consistent expertise and is well suited to environments that require ongoing, specialized support.

  • Long-term application needs
