Why Do You Need Dynamics 365 Customer Insights?

Customer information and data are scattered across multiple areas, starting from CRM, ERP, and more. Dynamics 365 Customer Insights, which was formerly known as Dynamics 365 Marketing, bridges that gap by bringing all the data under one roof.

Aegis Softtech helps you solve these business challenges:

Data trapped across disconnected systems

Since customer information is isolated across multiple business systems, it often leads to duplication and inconsistency. Now, without one source of record, your teams keep struggling with incomplete or unreliable customer details.

Broad, one-size-fits-all segmentation

Age or geography alone doesn't explain what actually drives behavior. Campaigns built on shallow segments land generic and get ignored.

Slow, manual reactions to customer behavior

A cart abandonment, a form fill—by the time someone notices and follows up, the moment's gone. Customers expect a response now, not three days later.

Marketing, sales, and service working from different data

When each team pulls from its own system, customers get an inconsistent experience depending on who they're talking to. Misalignment shows up as repeated questions and mixed messaging.

Little visibility into what's actually working

No centralized analytics, no clear read on which campaigns, segments, or channels are actually driving results. Decisions end up based on instinct rather than real engagement data, with teams often spending 25% more time on outreach and reporting.

Manual, time-consuming campaign work

Building segments, writing content, and assembling journeys by hand takes time away from strategy. More effort is spent on execution instead of upgrading the customer journey without proper AI assistance. Now that same effort, which is left unattended, can take up to 75% more time in comparison to an automated method.

Features of Dynamics 365 Customer Insights

Your customer data is scattered across multiple business tools that are not unified. Dynamics Customer Insights fetches all this data together to build a uniform and thorough customer portfolio.

Unified customer profiles

CRM, ERP, web, and service data together into one 360° view per customer. No more siloed records, no more guessing which system has the real answer.

Dynamic segmentation

Build audiences from behavior, purchase history, and preferences. Segments update themselves as the data changes; no manual refresh, no stale lists.

Omnichannel journeys

Email, SMS, push, event invites — one canvas, one journey, no switching between tools to keep the story consistent across channels.

Real-time triggers

A website visit, a cart abandonment, a form fill: respond the moment it happens, not on the next campaign cycle.

Analytics & Power BI

Engagement, conversion, and journey performance in built-in dashboards. Or push it straight into Power BI if that's where your team already lives.

A/B testing

Run different content, messages, and journey paths against each other. Keep what converts, cut what doesn't.

Microsoft ecosystem integration

Native with D365 Sales, Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Teams, Outlook, Power Platform, Fabric, and Azure. No middleware, no workarounds.

Enterprise security & scale

Role-based access, encryption, auditing built on Microsoft's cloud, sized for teams of any scale.
