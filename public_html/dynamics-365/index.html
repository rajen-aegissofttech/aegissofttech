Industries We Support Through Our Dynamics 365 Consulting Services

Manufacturing

Retail & eCommerce

One loyalty program. One inventory view. A customer can shop online or walk into a store, but we make sure the systems behind the scenes stay in sync.

Healthcare

Care coordination breaks down in the gaps between appointments and follow-ups. We close those gaps with a single system that keeps care teams aligned.

Pharma & Life Sciences

Distributor tracking and compliance usually live apart, and that's where audit risk builds up. We bring both into one system without slowing the supply chain down.

Banking & Financial Services

Compliance checks take time away from clients. Automating that process gives relationship teams more room to focus on people instead of paperwork.

Insurance

Most insurance claims delays trace back to a data problem, not a people problem. We fix the data flow so claims and policy updates move without manual chasing.

Public Sector

Citizens shouldn't have to follow up manually on their own paperwork. Structured case handling and automated document flow fix that.

Professional Services

Profitability erodes quietly: a few unbilled hours here, a delayed invoice there. Real-time utilization and billing visibility catch it before it adds up.

Education

Admissions, engagement, and alumni relations are often managed as three separate systems. We connect the full student journey so engagement doesn't stop at graduation.

Real Estate & Construction

Property portfolios and project timelines are closely linked but rarely managed together. We connect them, so decisions in one reflect what's happening in the other.

Frequently Asked Questions

It's Microsoft's suite of cloud-based CRM and ERP applications, covering sales, finance, customer service, operations, and supply chain — all built to work from a connected platform rather than as separate tools.

CRM is responsible for managing the client-facing functions of an organization, such as Sales, Marketing and Service. ERP is concerned with what's underneath the scenes: accounting, stock, production, and supply chain. Most organisations end up managing both, as both facets of the business seldom function independently.

It can. Dynamics 365 connects to Microsoft's own product ecosystem as well as a wide range of third-party applications, so data moves across systems without someone manually exporting and re-entering it.

It scales well for that reason specifically. Companies typically start with just the applications they need right now, then add users, modules, AI features, or integrations later — no need to commit to the full platform upfront.

Yes. It runs on Microsoft Azure and supports GDPR, ISO 27001, HIPAA, SOC — role-based access, encryption, compliance management built in, not bolted on for industries like finance and healthcare that actually need it.
