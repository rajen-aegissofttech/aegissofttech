Business Challenges We Help Solve

Most businesses don’t come to us because Dynamics 365 is broken. In fact, they come to us because it’s not working for them. Here’s what we hear often and what we offer as part of our Dynamics 365 CRM customization services.

Dynamics 365 doesn't fit your workflows

You're bending your process to match the software instead of the other way round. We reshape the system around how your team already works, not the reverse.

Too many manual processes

Someone's still copying data between screens or updating the same record in three places. We automate the repetitive stuff so your team spends time on work that actually needs a person.

Low CRM adoption

The system’s live, but your sales team barely opens it. If there is an attempt to work around it, something must be wrong in the process. We streamline the interface until it is usable again.

Multiple disconnected systems

When your CRM gives you one figure while your ERP gives you another, there is no telling what is correct. We interlink the two systems to ensure that data flows automatically.

Complex approval workflows

Approvals sit in someone's inbox for days. What should take an hour takes weeks. We rebuild the flow so that requests route automatically and deals stop stalling.

Reporting limitations

You need answers, but pulling a decent report means exporting to Excel and fixing it manually. We set up dashboards that show what you need, when you need it.

Industry-specific requirements

Generic CRM setups don't account for how your industry runs and what you need. Your industry requirements, compliance rules, or unique steps- none of these fit a default template. We build for your industry, not a generic solution.

Customization vs. Configuration: What's the Difference?

Two different ways to shape Dynamics 365

Comparison FactorConfigurationCustomization
DefinitionUses built-in Dynamics 365 features without custom coding.Extends Dynamics 365 with custom code, plugins, APIs, and integrations.
Best ForBusinesses with standard processes and minimal changes.Businesses with unique, complex, or industry-specific requirements.
Development EffortLow; primarily point-and-click setup.Medium to high; requires development and technical expertise.
Implementation TimeFaster to deploy.Takes longer due to design, development, and testing.
FlexibilityLimited to out-of-the-box capabilities.Highly flexible and tailored to specific business needs.
MaintenanceEasier to maintain and upgrade.Requires ongoing maintenance and compatibility testing.
ExamplesCustom fields, forms, views, business rules, security roles.Plugins, custom workflows, APIs, integration with Power Automate, custom dashboards, portals, third-party integration.
Typical ScenarioCustomization of Dynamics 365 to match typical sales, service, or finance procedures.Creating custom workflows, automating complicated processes, or integrating with enterprise-level systems.

Our advice: We begin a project with an analysis of what can be accomplished using configuration only; then we recommend customization where it is needed.
