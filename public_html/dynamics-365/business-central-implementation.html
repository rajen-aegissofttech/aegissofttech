Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central: The Concept

Dynamics 365 Business Central is a comprehensive, cloud-powered ERP solution designed to digitally unify your finance, operations, sales, supply chain, and service functions on a single platform.

Business Central is your growth engine.

Many growing businesses only scratch the surface of what Business Central can do, using it for basic accounting or reporting. In reality, it’s designed to streamline operations, automate workflows, and more.

To get the most out of it, you must look beyond surface-level deployment. Realize its full potential with the right setup, customizations, and user-centric processes.

When implemented right, Business Central (BC) delivers an ROI of up to 250%, with businesses seeing returns within 6 to 12 months of going live.

Learn more about Business Central.

Our Dynamics 365 Business Central Implementation Process

At Aegis Softtech, our approach to BC implementation is clean, error-free transitions that avoid costly pitfalls of poorly planned moves. It is designed to adapt to your business priorities and ensure the ERP solution works the way your teams do.

Get a detailed understanding of our Business Central implementation best practices:

Before any configuration begins, we focus on understanding your business inside out:

  • Requirements Gathering: Our experts engage with your stakeholders to map out key processes, pain points, and goals.
  • Gap Analysis: It involves a detailed analysis to identify where your current systems fall short and how Business Central can close those gaps.
  • Roadmap Design: You receive a clear, high-level implementation roadmap, tailored to your workflows, with defined KPIs and success metrics.
  • Project Plan: We finalize a detailed project plan, so you have full visibility of the scope, timelines, and milestones throughout the implementation process.

Our expert team gets to configuring, customizing, and integrating Business Central—always focused on your business outcomes. The process involves:

  • System Configuration: Tailored setup of core modules like Finance, Sales, Purchasing, Inventory, Manufacturing, and Projects. We configure features like users, security roles, and permissions.
  • Data Migration: At this stage, we plan and execute data migration from legacy systems like NAV, GP, QuickBooks, or SAP. Our experts cleanse and map data to ensure accuracy during the transfer and perform test migrations for data validation.
  • Custom Development: Where required, we build custom reports, workflows, and features. Our experts modify standard BC features using AI programming (for extensions) to create custom code to meet your business-specific requirements.
  • Uninterrupted Integrations: Be it CRMs, POS systems, warehouse management, eCommerce, or manufacturing systems, we ensure smooth integration of Business Central with your enterprise applications. Your tech stack stays connected and efficient.

We make sure your system is ready for real-world use by:

  • Unit & User Acceptance Testing (UAT): At every phase, we unit test each module to ensure accurate performance. UAT involves key users to validate that the BC system meets your requirements and performs as intended in a real-world scenario.
  • Hands-on training: We train your teams with easy-to-follow guides and “superuser” sessions, so your staff is confident to support the system post-implementation.

Our job isn’t done at go-live. We stay with you to ensure stability:

  • Go-live preparation: Validate data migration and verify integrations to take the implementation live. We address any immediate roadblocks or queries at this stage.
  • Go-live Support: We offer real-time support during the transition to ensure smooth operations. Any system performance issues are resolved immediately.
  • Post-go-live Assistance: We fine-tune, troubleshoot, and ensure your teams settle into the new system smoothly. You receive continued support, including bug fixes, system updates, and addressing any user concerns.
  • Periodic system health checks: It’s done to proactively address performance and operational concerns.

As your business evolves, we make the platform grow with you:

  • Ongoing Training: We conduct refresher training to help your teams learn about the advanced features over time.
  • System Updates and Upgrades: Our experts keep you aligned with Microsoft’s latest capabilities by implementing new features and functionalities as needed. It enables the system to meet business needs as your organization evolves.
  • Performance Tuning: We monitor system performance and optimize solutions to improve speed, reduce errors, and streamline processes.
  • New Features’ Implementation: Implement new features and modules so your system always supports your business goals and practices.

Our Business Central Implementation Deployment Options

We guide you through the right deployment strategy based on your business goals, industry regulations, and growth plans.

Explore the options below:

Cloud

Most of our clients today choose Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Cloud for its flexibility, lower infrastructure costs, and automatic updates. Microsoft rolls out two major updates annually—Wave 1 and Wave 2—introducing new features, performance improvements, and compliance upgrades.

With the cloud version, you always stay on the latest release without worrying about manual patching or downtime.

We ensure your custom workflows and integrations remain stable during each update cycle and schedule deployments during non-working hours to minimize disruption.

On-Premise

Microsoft is gradually phasing out on-premise versions for new customers, but we continue to support existing clients migrating from legacy systems like NAV or GP to Business Central On-Premise.

Our team ensures you have a secure, compliant setup, with full control over your data and infrastructure, especially in industries with strict regulatory requirements.
