Customer Billing

Dynamics 365 Project Operations Pricing & Licensing

Dynamics 365 Project Operations follows a per-user, subscription-based licensing model. The cost depends on the level of access users require and whether they already have a qualifying Dynamics 365 application.

LicenseList Price (USD)List Price (CAD)
Project Operations – Full User$135/user/month$183.20/user/month
Project Operations – Attach License$30/user/month$40.70/user/month
Team Member$8/user/month$10.90/user/month

Full User License

Project managers, resource managers, finance professionals running projects, resources, time, expenses, financial processes end to end — this is the license built for people actually doing that work, not glancing at it occasionally.

Attach License

Organizations with users who already have a qualifying Dynamics 365 base application may be able to add Project Operations at a lower attach-license price, subject to Microsoft licensing requirements.

Team Member License

Entering time and expenses, reviewing project information, the lightweight tasks that don't need full access: this covers that, and nothing more than that.

Note: Microsoft pricing, qualifying applications, licensing rights, and regional prices can change. Verify current Microsoft licensing terms and pricing before purchasing.
