Stakeholders come together in a brief discovery session to clarify needs and establish priorities, resulting in a clear plan with tasks, a timeline, and two-week sprint cycles.

02

Prototype & Blueprint Validation

A clickable, basic prototype and clear blueprint, showing screens, data, and integrations, are reviewed by stakeholders before development begins.

Your solution evolves in short 2-week sprints where scoped apps, features, workflows, and scripts are built. Each cycle includes demos and feedback sessions to keep the progress transparent and adaptable.

FAQs

The ServiceNow development platform is a cloud-based technology. It is used for building apps and automating workflows across IT, HR, customer service, and more.

  • Build and customize applications on the ServiceNow platform.
  • Digitize workflows across departments.
  • ServiceNow platform consultation
  • Configure modules and write JavaScript code for custom features.
  • Test, troubleshoot, and maintain platform stability.
  • Balance coding with process understanding to deliver effective solutions.

There’s no single tool that outright replaces ServiceNow, but several ITSM and enterprise service management platforms offer similar capabilities.

The four pillars of ServiceNow are: Strategy, People, Processes, and Technology.

  • Strategy for setting clear goals
  • People focus on the right roles and skills,
  • Processes tell how work gets done efficiently, and
  • Technology for automation and integration.

There are three main types of ServiceNow workflows:

  • Incident Management: reporting and resolving IT issues
  • Change Management: requests, approvals, risk-free implementation of changes
  • Problem Management: Identifies root causes to prevent recurring issues
