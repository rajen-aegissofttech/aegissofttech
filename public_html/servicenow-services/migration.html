Challenges Aegis Softtech’s ServiceNow Migration Services Solve

Outdated systems, poor data quality, lack of integration, and compliance gaps can make it difficult to maintain and scale your IT environment. A ServiceNow migration can be used to resolve these issues and build a more secure, connected, and scalable platform. Below are some of the most common migration challenges we help solve:

Complex Legacy Systems

Old customizations and legacy configurations can hinder new functionalities or security fixes, making a ServiceNow legacy migration more complex.

Data Quality Issues

Inconsistent, duplicated, and messy data can cause delays in migration and hinder daily workflows.

Integration Breakdowns

Disconnected CRMs, ERPs, or any other business tool can create silos and limit the effectiveness of ServiceNow.

Downtime Risks

During mergers and business restructuring, system outages can lead to downtime, affecting your team’s productivity and daily operations.

Audit Gaps

Businesses with strict governance and compliance requirements cannot afford gaps in audit trails or data management.

Scalability Concerns

Scaling can become difficult when on-premises systems are not set up to handle increasing workloads.

Our ServiceNow Migration Strategies Built Around Your Environment

We firmly believe that not all migrations have to be alike. This is why each ServiceNow migration is tailored to the approach that would fit best within your environment and your needs.

Full data load

Every historical record moves into ServiceNow. Best when your data volume is manageable, and you need complete historical continuity from day one.

Phased load with archive

Recent and active data gets loaded into ServiceNow right away; older data gets moved into low-cost archive storage with on-demand lookup. Best for large estates where most queries only ever touch recent data.

Parallel run

Your old system and new system operate simultaneously over a set period of time as teams are transitioned into the process, reducing the risk of a hard cutover. Best when your team can’t absorb a single-day switch.

Phased by module or business unit

You begin by migrating ITSM and then proceed to migrate other modules such as CSM and HRSD, among others. Best for large or multi-department organizations that need to prove value before scaling further.

How We Deliver a Successful ServiceNow Migration Process

A structured ServiceNow migration process helps identify risks early, verify what is being migrated, and reduce problems during cutover. Aegis Softtech’s approach covers six stages from initial assessment through post-migration support.

01

Assessment & Planning

Define the migration scope, success metrics, the key stakeholders, and the timeline to ensure that everyone is aware of what will migrate and when.

02

Audit & Risk Assessment

Conduct a review of integrations, customizations, data quality, and CMDB gaps. The results of the audit can be used to understand possible risks for the migration and what needs to be addressed before execution.

03

Build & Prepare

Test all necessary automation, workflows, applications, and configurations in a non-production environment before moving to production. If you are planning to implement a new ServiceNow setup, our ServiceNow implementation services can support configuration, workflows, integrations, and deployment.

04

Execute the Migration

Conduct the migration in controlled phases. Where appropriate, parallel runs or synchronization can be used to validate the target environment prior to the complete migration process.

05

Test & Validate

Run a pilot migration and perform unit testing and user acceptance testing (UAT) to validate data, processes, and platform performance prior to going live.

06

Post-Migration Support

Provide hypercare and knowledge transfer after migration. This engagement can also involve a closing assessment to compare migration results against agreed KPIs.

For continuous monitoring, maintenance, optimization, and support of the platform post-migration, explore our ServiceNow managed services.

FAQ About ServiceNow Migration

ServiceNow migration services help businesses move data, workflows, integrations, reports, configurations, and other required components from a legacy system to ServiceNow or between ServiceNow environments. The scope can include assessment, planning, data preparation, migration, testing, validation, and post-migration support.

ServiceNow data migration is the process of transferring data from one environment to ServiceNow or between ServiceNow environments. This includes data mapping, data cleansing, transformation, de-duplication, transfer, and validation so it remains accurate and usable after migration.

We support migrations from different legacy ITSM, help desk, and enterprise platforms. This can include Cherwell to ServiceNow migration and Jira to ServiceNow migration, with the approach planned around your existing data, workflows, integrations, and configurations.

Yes. A ServiceNow legacy migration starts with assessing the existing data, integrations, customizations, dependencies, and business processes. The migration approach then depends on the source system and what needs to be moved.

Some of the popular ServiceNow migration approaches include full migration, phased migration, parallel migration, and migration of modules/business units. The best ServiceNow migration method depends on a number of factors including data volume, system complexity, downtime tolerance, business requirements, and migration timeline.

Risk of data loss during migration can be minimized by data cleansing, mapping, validation, controlled migration, pilot runs, testing, and reconciliation. Alternatively, a rollback and recovery strategy may also be planned depending on the requirements of the migration.

ServiceNow migration testing can include pilot migration, data checks, unit testing, integration testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), and workflow testing. Testing allows identifying any issues before the complete environment is moved to production.

The timeline depends on the amount of data, number of integrations, customizations, modules in use, testing requirements, and migration strategy, among others. The size of the migration will also determine how much time it takes.

The cost of ServiceNow migration services depends on the source and target environment, amount of data, integrations, customizations, complexity of migration process, testing requirements, project timeline, and the engagement model. Conducting a migration assessment helps understand the scope and estimate the costs.

After migration, the target environment is validated to confirm that data, workflows, integrations, and other migrated components are working as expected. Post-migration services may also include hypercare, issue resolution, performance monitoring, knowledge transfer, and optimization.
