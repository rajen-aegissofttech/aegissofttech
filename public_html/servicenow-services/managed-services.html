Aegis Softtech ServiceNow Managed Services

Aegis Softtech’s ServiceNow managed services provide more than just fixing any issues as they arise; they provide day-to-day administration, maintenance, monitoring, enhancements, integrations, reporting, and platform improvements needed to keep your ServiceNow environment running effectively.

ServiceNow Platform Administration

Keep everyday platform administration from consuming your internal team's time. We take care of user provisioning/deprovisioning, roles and groups, system properties, email notifications, form changes, UI policies, client scripts, and routine instance management.

We also support multi-instance environments across production, development, testing, and QA, with all changes tracked, tested, and promoted through the appropriate lifecycle using update sets or application repository deployments.

Key areas:

User & access managementRoles & groupsCore configurationUI policiesClient scriptsInstance managementMulti-instance management

ServiceNow Support Services and Incident Resolution

Get ServiceNow customer services and platform issues to the right level of expertise using a well-defined support and escalation framework. Level 1 deals with initial triage and known issues; Level 2 includes technical specialists and ServiceNow developers for break/fix and enhancement; while Level 3 includes senior architects for any problem relating to the platform or integration. Service Delivery Manager is the primary point of contact for any service issues and escalation.

How Aegis Softtech Manages Your ServiceNow Environment

ServiceNow managed services solutions should not just be about keeping the platform operational. Here's what we offer you:

Monitoring & Health Checks

Managed services should not begin only when someone raises a ticket. Our proactive monitoring model combines continuous platform monitoring with scheduled health and performance reviews.

  • Daily Health Checks
    Automated and manual checks of system logs, scheduled jobs, and integration queues.
  • Weekly Performance Reviews
    Analysis of performance trends to identify potential bottlenecks.
  • Instance Observer Dashboard
    A custom dashboard provides a consolidated, real-time view of key platform-health metrics, including slow queries, scheduler performance, and integration health.
  • Quarterly Health Assessments
    A deeper review of the instance configuration against ServiceNow best practices.
  • Platform Analytics
    Platform Analytics can be configured to track performance trends such as incident resolution times, catalog request fulfillment, and change success rates.
  • Event Management
    For clients with an ITOM license, Event Management can be configured to create alerts from monitoring tools before they become incidents.

Governance, Reporting & Continuous Improvement

Ongoing ServiceNow management needs clear ownership, regular communication, and visibility into both current performance and upcoming platform priorities. Our managed-services framework involves:

  • Weekly Operational Meeting
    Review ticket backlogs, prioritize tasks, and address immediate blockers.
  • Monthly Service Review
    Review service performance against agreed SLAs with the Service Delivery Manager.
  • Quarterly Business Review
    Review SLA performance and key metrics, enhancements delivered, the upcoming platform roadmap, new features, and recommendations for process improvement and greater ROI.
  • Platform Roadmap
    Plan upgrades in advance with defined testing and communication requirements. New ServiceNow features can also be reviewed through value-add workshops to determine where they may benefit the business and whether adoption should be planned.
  • KPIs We Track
    SLA Compliance Rate • Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR) • Incident Backlog Trend • Change Success Rate • Self-Service Adoption Rate • Platform Uptime & Availability • CMDB Accuracy / Staleness Rate

Our ServiceNow Managed Services Approach

A standard managed-services transition follows a structured 60–90-day process designed to transfer platform knowledge and responsibilities in stages.

01

Discovery & Knowledge Transfer: Days 1–30

Review documentation, conduct workshops, understand the existing ServiceNow environment, and establish monitoring tools.

02

Shadowing & Execution: Days 31–60

Begin handling lower-priority tickets and tasks while shadowing the existing team on critical processes.

03

Full Service Takeover: Days 61–90

Assume responsibility for platform management, with the client's team acting as an escalation point. The Service Delivery Manager becomes fully engaged and regular reporting begins.

What's Outside Our ServiceNow Managed Services Scope?

A clear scope from ServiceNow support providers helps both teams know where managed services end and a separate ServiceNow project begins. The following activities are typically handled outside the managed-services scope.

Initial ServiceNow Implementation

The first-time ServiceNow implementation services or a new major module, such as implementing ITOM, ITAM, or HRSD from scratch.

Major Application Development

Building entirely new custom applications on ServiceNow App Engine.

Third-Party Software Management

We manage the integration to third-party systems such as SAP or Workday, but not the administration or maintenance of those systems themselves.

ServiceNow License Procurement

Clients are responsible for purchasing and maintaining their ServiceNow license entitlements.

Hardware & Network Infrastructure

Management of the underlying physical or cloud infrastructure is outside the managed-services scope.
