Signs You Need ServiceNow Consulting Services

ServiceNow problems rarely begin with the platform itself. They show up in day-to-day operations: a ServiceNow project falls behind schedule, upgrades get put off again and again, teams develop their own solutions without using ServiceNow, or tasks that should be straightforward end up being far more complicated than expected.

If you're seeing these issues regularly, it may be worth reassessing how your ServiceNow environment is set up.

Your ServiceNow project keeps stretching beyond its initial plan.

Your ServiceNow project, which was initially scoped out well, has now led to more requirements, integrations, customizations, and unresolved decisions. There are constantly new deadlines, but it is increasingly difficult to determine which changes are actually necessary to get the project back on track.

Upgrades have turned into something your team tries to avoid.

With every ServiceNow upgrade comes a fear of what could fail. The customizations must be checked, the integrations must be re-tested, and the dependencies make the effect hard to predict. Postponing the upgrades can be a sign that the instance has become unnecessarily complex.

People are doing their work “around” ServiceNow rather than within it.

Requests come via email. Approvals take place through instant messaging or over chat. Teams use spreadsheets along with ServiceNow because the current process is difficult or the information can’t be relied upon. The fact that work keeps getting done outside of the platform might indicate that there is some problem with adoption, processes, data, or configuration that needs to be addressed.

Your CMDB is there, but teams don't trust it.

The configuration records are present, but teams wonder about whether they are up-to-date, correct in their relationships, or have proper ownership defined. If people are verifying CMDB data elsewhere before taking any actions on it, data quality and governance need attention.

You're not using everything you're paying for.

You may be paying for ServiceNow modules and features that your teams rarely use. Low adoption can mean you're not getting the full value from your ServiceNow investment.

Your automation initiatives keep getting stuck.

The automation backlog keeps growing, but the same manual processes remain. Unclear process ownership, integration dependencies, complex workflows, or competing priorities can prevent automation initiatives from moving from ideas into production.

Teams in various roles are pushing ServiceNow in different directions.

IT departments, operations, and business teams might have their own requirements for the platform. In the absence of any sort of governance, workflows, applications, and customization efforts may cause ServiceNow to become a set of unrelated projects.

You know ServiceNow has more capabilities to offer, but you are not clear about where to start.

The platform is running, but the next step isn't clear. You may be unsure which capabilities, improvements, or new ServiceNow products should be prioritized. A ServiceNow consultant can assess the current environment and help identify where to focus next.
