Digital Agriculture Services

Agriculture has gone through a train of revolutions, most of which aimed to improve crop yield, productivity and profitability. Since the last decade, digitization in agriculture has brought an immense transformation in the way farmers grow crops and create multiple revenue streams.

Aegis offers comprehensive digital agriculture services that help farmers to make the most of the technology and exercise complete control over their yield. Let’s bridge the gap between the native agricultural methods and the modern smart digital farming solutions for maximum profits and efficiency.

Empowered Clients

Pick Your Choice of a Digital Farming Platform

While the digitization of agriculture began many years ago, only 20% of the agricultural land area is managed by digital agriculture solutions. At Aegis, we’re helping farmers to embrace the advantages of smart digital farming solutions and use digital agriculture platforms to increase the overall productivity and crop yield.

Farmers can address various inefficiencies in farming and employ innovative solutions to combat them. If this is your first time employing such agriculture solutions, we recommend choosing one digital farming platform.

Farm Management Software

This is one of the fundamental platforms that you’d require to get a peek into the improvements brought in by digitization. With this software, you can:

  • Manage crop irrigation
  • Automate crop watering
  • Monitor food safety
  • Manage customer relationships
  • Decide and evaluate pricing and inventory
  • Manage equipment maintenance

Farm Accounting Software

Unlike the common accounting software that needs so much effort to ace and understand, we develop customized software to manage your farm accounts, record the movement of funds and easily take stock of the revenue. With this farm accounting software, you can:

  • Forecast the need for goods
  • Keep track of the funds
  • Automate paying the suppliers
  • Receive payments online and balance the books easily

Agriculture Business Marketing Software

If you plan to run marketing campaigns for your agriculture business, you definitely need this software. You’ll have access to every tool you need to:

  • Automate your marketing campaigns
  • Create targeted marketing strategies
  • Gather insights from the marketing efforts
  • Streamline the conversion funnel

Livestock Management Software

When you have livestock of different breeds and varieties, it can be confusing to keep account of everything. This is why our tailored livestock management software offers a central system of livestock management. With this software, you can:

  • Track animals and breeding
  • Use RFID sensors or any other sensors to keep track of your livestock
  • Manage breeding for optimized livestock production at reduced rates
  • Direct the livestock towards green and luscious grazing fields

Custom Agriculture Software

Do you want to test the soil? Try out some innovative agricultural practices? For any such innovative strategies you involve, our custom agriculture solutions and management software will help gain deep insights.

  • Soil mapping
  • Remote tracking
  • Usage of drones and IoT
  • Crop sensor data collections
  • 3D field view and simulation
