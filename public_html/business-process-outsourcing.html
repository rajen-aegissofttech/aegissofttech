FAQs

Business process outsourcing means collaborating with a third-party vendor or sub-contractor to perform some operations of the business activities, particularly the non-core ones, like bookkeeping, medical billing and coding, and others.

Here are some factors that you should keep in mind while looking for a BPO service company in India:

  • Their experience and their journey will help ensure whether their services are aligned with your business needs or not.
  • Validate the company's claims. Check the reviews and contact past clients.
  • Check out their problem-solving skills and technical infrastructure. You can evaluate by browsing their case studies.
  • Talk about their staff turnover rate, which is essential to maintain a stable team for your project.
  • Evaluate their ability to scale up and whether they can accommodate your growing needs.

The name for BPO services changes depending upon the outsourced services. If the business process is information technology-based, then BPO services are referred to as ITES-BPO, where ITES stands for Information Technology Enabled Services.

There are three common BPO pricing models: hourly-based, transaction-based, and FTE-based (full-time equivalent).

Here are some signs that indicate that your business should recruit a BPO service company:

  • You have to reduce the increasing operational costs.
  • With the in-house team, you can achieve the desired level of productivity and efficiency.
  • Your business is growing, and you will require experts to accommodate the increasing needs.
  • The non-core activities are affecting your core business activities.
  • Your existing team is overworking or overwhelmed due to excess workload.
