FAQs

2025 has an emerging trend and necessity of integrating AI into businesses. AI integration is upgrading your existing and current systems with AI features to make them intelligent and more efficient.

To integrate AI into existing products, we follow a step-by-step process as the following,

Identify Application cases

Identify where AI can truly make a difference, for example: creating personalized experiences, automating tasks, or analyzing data smarter and faster.

Prepare the Data

We collect and simplify data for AI model training. The process involves collecting, cleaning, and labeling data to keep it ready for the next step.

Pick the Right Tools

Choose AI platforms and technologies that fit your goals, whether it’s powerful libraries like TensorFlow or cloud-based services for seamless integration.

Build the Models

Train custom AI models with your data or use pre-trained ones to save time and resources.

Seamless Integration

Add AI features to your product, ensuring everything works together smoothly and fits your existing setup.

Test and Improve

Run the AI features with real users, get their feedback, and make tweaks to enhance performance and usability.

Monitor regularly

Our team monitors AI performance regularly, making updates and adjustments.

The total budget for integrating AI into the product depends on several factors:

Project Complexity

Simple AI tools like chatbots are affordable, but custom AI models and advanced predictive systems can raise costs.

Required Features

Adding advanced functionalities like machine learning, NLP, or computer vision increases development expenses.

Data Readiness

If your business data is well-organized, costs are lower. However, preparing and cleaning data can add to the expense.

Team Expertise & Time

Experienced teams charge more but deliver higher quality. Simpler projects take less time and cost less than complex ones.

Platforms & Technology

Costs vary based on whether AI is integrated into Android, iOS, or cloud-based systems. Platform updates can also increase expenses.

Maintenance & Support

The ongoing costs are added for Post-launch, regular updates, bug fixes, and performance monitoring.
