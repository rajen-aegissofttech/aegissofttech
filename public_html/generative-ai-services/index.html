Our Generative AI Development Services

As a trusted generative AI development company, we offer end-to-end custom generative AI development services that cover strategy, development, deployment, integration, and continuous optimization.

Here's how our generative AI services help you transform ideas into production-ready AI solutions.

1. Generative AI Consultancy & Strategy

Before any build starts, we figure out which use case pays for itself first: scoring, data readiness, model selection, a realistic cost-to-value roadmap. That order matters more than the tech stack.

2. Custom Generative AI Development

Your processes are unique; a generic AI tool isn't built for them. We build around your workflows, data, and compliance requirements instead of asking your operations to adapt to the tool.

3. AI Agent Development

We build agents that handle multi-step processes without losing governance along the way — approval workflows, operational boundaries, performance monitoring, all built in, not bolted on after something goes wrong.

4. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Development

Enterprise knowledge is only useful if people can actually find it. RAG turns your documentation, policies, and product information into natural-language answers, with sources attached, instead of a search bar that returns forty tabs.

5. AI Chatbot Development

Support, HR, IT service desks: chatbots that understand context, pull from your actual knowledge base, and know when to hand off instead of guessing.

6. LLM Integration Services

An AI model is only as useful as what it's connected to. We integrate GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, and Mistral into your CRM, ERP, and existing data platforms, built for security and scale from the start.

7. Generative AI Model Fine-Tuning

Generic models don't know your terminology or your compliance requirements. We fine-tune on your actual data, so responses need less manual review and stay consistent across business-critical work.

8. AI Integration and Workflow Automation

AI that sits outside the workflow doesn't get used. We embed it into support, document processing, approvals, and internal operations, so it becomes part of the process instead of another tab open next to it.

9. AI Support & Continuous Optimization

Deployment is the start, not the finish. We monitor performance, retrain as the business changes, and keep the system accurate instead of letting it quietly drift out of date.

Generative AI Security, Governance & Compliance

Aegis Softtech builds generative AI solutions with security, compliance, and responsible AI practices built in from the start, not bolted on after. Governance controls run across the full AI lifecycle: protecting data, managing risk, and keeping every decision accountable to someone.

01

GDPR

Data privacy, consent, and regulatory requirements get designed into the solution, not addressed after deployment.

02

HIPAA

Protected health information stays secured throughout, for any AI application touching healthcare data.

03

Secure AI Architecture

Secure data flows, access controls, encryption — built at the architecture level, not added as a patch later.

04

Human-in-the-Loop Validation

Critical decisions still go through a person. AI proposes; it doesn't auto-execute.

05

Bias Detection

We evaluate outputs for bias across use cases, so model reliability doesn't quietly degrade for some users and not others.

06

AI Guardrails

Controls restrict what the model can do — unsafe, inaccurate, or unauthorized behavior gets stopped before it happens.

07

Monitoring

We track performance, usage, and accuracy continuously after deployment, not just at launch.

08

Audit Trails

Every AI interaction and decision leaves a traceable record, so accountability doesn't depend on memory.

Which Engagement Model Is Right for Your Business?

No two AI initiatives look the same, so the engagement model shouldn't be fixed either — new build, growing an existing team, or a long-term AI capability, we shape around whichever it is.

Dedicated AI Team

Architects, engineers, data scientists, QA, project managers: working on your AI initiatives and nothing else. Makes sense when there's more than one use case, or a roadmap that runs past this year.

Best for: long-term AI programs, enterprise transformation, multiple initiatives.

Project-Based Development

One solution, a real deadline, a real budget. This model runs discovery through deployment and post-launch support, with milestones you can actually plan around.

Best for: proofs of concept, MVPs, end-to-end solution builds.

Team Augmentation

Your engineering team, plus AI specialists who fit into how you already work — engineers, data scientists, prompt engineers, MLOps, whichever gap needs filling. Faster than hiring, without the long-term headcount.

Best for: in-house teams that need specialized AI expertise on demand.
