Our Capabilities In Tableau Products

Tableau Next

Tableau Next uses AI and Agentforce to deliver smart, personalized insights. With Tableau Next expertise, we deliver frictionless, multi-modal, conversational analytics helping businesses do more with their data. Our expertise in Tableau Next includes,

  • Implementation of Tableau semantics for AI data modeling,
  • Use Agent Force’s prebuilt analytics skills to enhance AI agents,
  • Set up Data Pro for data cleaning and visualization,
  • Enable concierge for natural data queries, and
  • Deploy an inspector to detect anomalies.

Tableau Cloud

Tableau Cloud is a fully hosted cloud-based analytical platform that helps businesses make quick and easy data-driven decisions allowing teams to analyze and act on data from anywhere.


We use Tableau Cloud features like Tableau Pulse, Embedded Analytics, and Slack integration to make insights easily accessible in daily workflows, unifying different facets of the organization, and maximizing your tableau investment.

Tableau Server

Tableau Server is a self-hosted analytics platform that helps businesses securely manage, share, and analyze data while maintaining full control over deployment and access.


We help businesses deploy self-service analytics with unmatched control & flexibility. We cover everything from setup, and integration, to optimizing the Tableau Server, and deploying it on-premises or in the cloud.

Tableau Desktop

Tableau Desktop helps businesses access, view, and understand data easily. It lets you quickly find important insights through drag-and-drop options, even if you are offline.


We help businesses get the best from Tableau Desktop by setting it up, boosting its speed, and making sure it connects easily with your data. Our Experts provide training, customize dashboards, and implement AI-powered analytics for deeper insights boosting the ROI.
