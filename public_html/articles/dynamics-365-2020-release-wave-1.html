Microsoft has launched 2026 release wave 1 on 1st of April which includes new applications and as well as around 400 new features across both Dynamics 365 sales Services and Power Platform.

Considering Covid-19 outbreak, Microsoft has delayed the deprecation of legacy web client by 2 months to December also the mandatory upgrade is also pushed by one month, i.e. May instead of April.

In this article you will learn how to create Dynamics 365 trial and how to upgrade it to the 2026 Release Wave 1.

To create the Dynamics 365 Trial

Navigate to trial portal https://trials.dynamics.com/

  • Select the Dynamics 365 App, here we are selecting Sales app to begin with.

  • To create the trail either you can use your work email or you can use your personal email id.

    To use your personal email id, click on Sign up here link and select No, continue signing up option

    Provide the appropriate details and click on Next to start the setup

  • Specify the username along with domain id and password. Give the consent as appropriate and click on Create my account.

  • The setup should complete take around 5 minutes or so.

  • Now to upgrade it to the 2026 Release Wave 1, you need to go to the Power Platform administration website. While these steps demonstrate the manual process, many organizations rely on Dynamics 365 upgrade services to minimize downtime and ensure compatibility with existing customizations.

    Go to Environments and open the environment to be upgraded

  • Inside the Updates section, click on Manage

  • Click on Update now to update the environment to the latest version.
