How to Leverage Dynamics CRM in the Financial Services Industry

  1. Home
  2. Articles
  3. How to Leverage Dynamics CRM in the Financial Services Industry

Introduction

In the competitive financial sector, strong client relationships are everything. How can financial institutions use technology to improve loyalty and engagement? Dynamics CRM offers powerful capabilities to connect with customers in new ways.

Let’s explore how to maximize this platform across retail banking, insurance, investments, and more.

Centralize Information for Personable Service

In the financial sector, providing personalized and contextual service is crucial for building long-term client relationships. However, vital customer information often ends up scattered across various systems and channels.

Customer service agents struggle to access complete histories, while advisors lack holistic views of their client's financial lives.

Dynamics CRM solves this by unifying client data into a single source. Agents and advisors gain 360-degree customer profiles that include every interaction, service case, product holding, life event, preference and more. Before integrating customer information into CRM systems, businesses can also use CRM Cleaning to identify and correct duplicate, outdated, or inconsistent records.

With this consolidated information at their fingertips, employees can have informed and relevant conversations that demonstrate a deep understanding of each client.

For example, an advisor meeting with a client can reference their profile to bring up recent life events or financial goals. This level of personalization makes clients feel valued.

A service agent can instantly pull up account transaction history and details to provide customized assistance. Centralizing data is the foundation for next-level personal service that drives loyalty.

Streamline Lead and Opportunity Management

Finances are a competitive space. The ability to effectively capitalize on leads and opportunities can make or break business performance. For industries like finance, leveraging advanced technology for financial advisors helps streamline data analysis, improve client insights, and drive smarter investment decisions.

Too often, promising prospects fall through the cracks when they are not nurtured quickly enough. Other times, clunky opportunity management processes cause deals to stall.

Dynamics CRM optimizes the lead-to-opportunity pipeline by applying automation and intelligence.

Features like lead scoring help identify and flag high-potential prospects from web forms, events, referrals and other sources. Workflows can then automatically schedule timely follow-up emails and calls to make contact when interest is hottest.

Once a lead is sales-ready, predefined rules ensure they are routed to the right sales reps. From there, the opportunity progresses through a customized pipeline with built-in triggers for key stages.

Activity reminders, templates and playbooks keep the deal moving efficiently toward close. Sales managers gain visibility into conversion rates and bottlenecks to refine processes.

The result is a lean, data-driven engine for accelerating opportunities and increasing conversions. Financial institutions that leverage these capabilities in Dynamics CRM pull ahead of the competition. They can also utilize SAP S/4HANA public cloud to enhance operational efficiency by providing real-time access to financial, supply chain, and ERP data, supporting better decision-making and process optimization.

Deliver Omnichannel Customer Experiences

Today's financially savvy customers expect seamless service across all channels - whether they engage via the website, mobile app, call center, in-person appointment or other touchpoints. Firms must deliver complete omnichannel experiences to remain competitive.

Dynamics CRM provides the tools to achieve this consistency by unifying context across channels.

When a customer calls the service center after interacting online, agents can immediately view their web activity history to continue the conversation. Email interactions integrate with web behavioral data for coherent nurturing. An effective omnichannel contact center ensures that every customer interaction is connected and consistent, no matter the channel used.

The key is maintaining a single view of the customer that moves between channels. Dynamics CRM serves as the hub for aggregating data and keeping teams connected.

With this foundation, financial institutions can achieve true omnichannel capabilities that meet rising consumer demands. Multi-channel delivery stops feeling like fragmented, inconsistent experiences from the customer's perspective.

Simplify Client Onboarding and Retention

Beyond new business, financial services firms must also focus on smoothly onboarding clients and retaining them for the long term. Dynamics CRM shines here as well with robust features catered to each goal.

For onboarding, predefined workflows standardize tasks like paperwork collection, account configuration and client team introductions. Checklists and templates ensure consistency while automating repetitive steps.

With an efficient and polished onboarding sequence, new clients get up and running quickly while having an excellent first impression.

Retaining clients requires being proactive. Dynamics CRM identifies at-risk customers based on engagement drops, sentiment analysis, churn predictors and other data sources.

Tailored playbooks can then trigger personalized outreach such as service incentives or redemption offers before its too late. Tracking loyalty program status and automatically enrolling or elevating tiers based on activity also increases retention.

With CRM capabilities purpose-built for the financial technology sector, institutions can execute polished onboarding and informed retention practices that strengthen client relationships over time.

Create holistic client profiles

In most financial institutions, client information lives fragmented across various systems - web data in one place, in-person meeting notes in another, and transaction history elsewhere. This prevents complete understanding.

Dynamics CRM solves this by unifying data into comprehensive 360-degree client profiles. These consolidated profiles include full account timelines, service cases, product holdings, marketing response history, advisor meeting notes about goals and life events, and more.

With all data sources integrated into one view, advisors and service teams get the full picture to drive personalization.

They can reference in-depth profiles during meetings for tailored recommendations based on complete financial lives, not just isolated tidbits. Complete profiles enable previously impossible tailored service by eliminating data silos.

Equip staff with full context

Nothing frustrates customers’ more than re-explaining details every time they interact with new staff. Dynamics CRM prevents this by equipping employees with shared context across departments.

When sales hands off a new client to onboarding, the entire CRM record seamlessly transfers over. The onboarding team picks up right where sales left off rather than restarting conversations from scratch.

The same goes for any transition between service, marketing and sales. Advisors and service staff also have full mobile access to profiles and history during meetings for smooth, informed discussions.

With visibility into the next steps and background, employees deliver consistent and personal assistance. Dynamics CRM facilitates seamless behind-the-scenes handoffs that customers appreciate.

Identify and capture leads

In financial services, many promising leads come from sources like website inquiries, event sign-ups, referrals, etc. But without prompt follow-up, these leads often slip away.

Dynamics CRM solves this through automated lead capture rules. Any prospect contact info submitted via forms, email sign-ups, events and more automatically creates a new lead record. Intelligent workflows then schedule immediate follow-up tailored to lead source - email nurtures, callback tasks, assignments to reps, etc.

No time is lost as hot prospects enter the pipeline for prompt contact. Quality leads rarely stay warm for long, so triggering instant outreach is critical for capitalizing on interest.

Microsoft Dynamics CRM Solutions help financial firms capture more opportunities through automated lead-capture capabilities.

Assign and progress opportunities

Once a promising lead is sales-ready after initial contact, Dynamics CRM ensures they get routed to the optimal sales rep based on client profile, product fit and availability. Assigned reps can progress the deal through a customized pipeline journey.

Activity reminders, call scripts, email templates and playbooks keep progress tracking on target through different sales stages without dropping the ball. Management visibility into performance metrics also enables data-driven process optimization.

By applying automation and intelligence to opportunity assignment and management, financial institutions progress more deals smoothly from lead to close. Dynamics CRM strengthens revenue pipelines.

Enable consistent service across channels

Today's clients use a variety of channels - web, mobile, phone, in-person, social, etc. They expect seamless service experiences regardless of how they engage. Dynamics CRM provides the cross-channel integration and visibility needed to enable this consistency.

Shared customer records maintain unified context across touchpoints so conversations flow smoothly between channels. A customer service agent can immediately view relevant web interactions to assist a caller without missing a beat.

Email campaigns integrate with website behavioral data for coordinated omnichannel nurturing.

Dynamics CRM is the connective tissue linking channels through shared customer intelligence. Financial firms can now meet rising omnichannel service demands.

Orchestrate personalized marketing

Personalized, tailored marketing across channels depends on unified customer data. Dynamics CRM enables this by integrating with marketing platforms.

Email campaigns, social ads, website experiences, and more can be orchestrated across channels based on shared Dynamics data. Customer journeys drive cross-channel messaging with personalized content delivered via marketing automation.

Dynamics provides the complete intelligence needed to coordinate omnichannel marketing. Financial services companies can enhance consistency and relevance across web, mobile, social and offline touchpoints with CRM-powered marketing automation.

Streamline onboarding

Smooth onboarding makes client retention easier, but disjointed manual processes often lead to poor experiences. Dynamics CRM provides consistency through standardized onboarding workflows.

These workflows automatically initiate key tasks like paperwork collection, document verification, account configuration, product training, and client team introductions on schedule. Checklists and templates ensure no step is missed.

With an automated, polished onboarding sequence, new customers get running quickly with an excellent first impression that builds loyalty foundations. Consistent processes driven by Dynamics CRM eliminate fragmented onboarding experiences.

Proactively manage retention

Beyond onboarding, Dynamics CRM enables proactive retention management by identifying at-risk accounts. Warning signs like low engagement, negative feedback and churn predictors trigger automated save tactics.

Playbooks can kick off personalized outreach, such as service credits or special offers, to re-engage clients before it’s too late. Targeted incentives driven by CRM data minimize preventable attrition.

Rather than just reacting when customers leave, Dynamics CRM powers proactive retention via contextual CX automation. Financial firms can preemptively boost engagement based on intelligence.

Reward loyalty

Loyalty programs help retain clients, but cumbersome administration often limits impact. Manually tracking tiers, points balances, and sending updates is resource-intensive.

Dynamics CRM optimizes loyalty programs by automating tedious processes. Eligibility criteria trigger automatic tier promotions, milestone announcements, and reward deliveries to the right customers at the right time.

With the CRM managing logistics behind the scenes, loyalty programs plugin enhance retention with minimal hassle. Customers feel valued through timely recognition while staff avoids manual upkeep. The program delivers maximum impact on loyalty.

Top Reasons to Choose Dynamics CRM

With its robust functionality designed for financial services, it’s clear why Dynamics CRM is the top choice for so many institutions seeking an enterprise-grade customer relationship management platform. Here are key advantages that set Dynamics CRM apart:

1) Role-based Interfaces

One key advantage of Dynamics CRM is its ability to present personalized experiences tailored to each employee's specific job duties through role-based interfaces.

Rather than a one-size-fits-all system, Dynamics CRM gives salespeople, service agents, marketers, managers, and other roles a custom view. The UI limits visibility to only the data points, features, and actions relevant to that particular user's responsibilities.

For example, a service agent may see key customer details and history along with ancillary tools to look up knowledge articles or log a case. However, complex pricing and discounting capabilities meant for the sales role are hidden from their view.

Meanwhile, a sales manager can access reports and dashboards on team performance without clutter from individual customer records. Marketing specialists get a project workflow view without distracting service tools.

This targeted approach prevents information overload. Employees can simply focus on core tasks without hunting through irrelevant features or data. Personalized UIs lead to higher productivity and role clarity. Recognizing employees who excel in their roles with ideal employee trophies fosters motivation and reinforces the value of efficiency, ultimately contributing to higher productivity and job satisfaction.

2) 360-Degree Customer Intelligence

Dynamics CRM delivers complete, unified profiles by integrating data across all systems into a single view. This consolidates every interaction and transaction related to a customer for unmatched context. Employees gain access to full histories and a holistic perspective to drive personalization.

3) Intelligent Automation

Sophisticated workflow automation and built-in AI take care of high-volume repetitive tasks while infusing intelligence into processes. Lead scoring, customer sentiment analysis, churn predictions, and other smart features ensure critical actions are taken at the right times based on data.

4) Omnichannel Consistency

Shared customer records across service, sales and marketing channels enable coordinated messaging and experiences. Dynamics CRM provides the connective tissue to link web, social, call centers, mobile, email and offline interactions into seamless journeys.

5) Agility and Scalability

As an enterprise cloud solution, Dynamics CRM enables quick deployment and ongoing enhancements as needs change. Custom entities, fields, modules and apps expand functionality over time. Usage-based licensing allows cost-effective scaling.

6) Ecosystem of Integrations

An extensive marketplace of turnkey app integrations makes Dynamics CRM extensible to other business systems. Prebuilt connectors exist for popular platforms like Office 365, LinkedIn, Adobe and more.

7) Security and Compliance

Dynamics CRM enables financial institutions to meet stringent regulatory compliance demands including data privacy, retention policies, and access controls. Cashless payment systems help to prevent unauthorized access and improve security. Native security features like encryption, auditing, and role-based access keep sensitive customer information protected.

8) Analytical Insights

Built-in business intelligence tools empower data-driven decisions across sales, marketing, and service. By integrating Strategic Foresight, these tools go beyond tracking real-time KPIs like sales pipeline velocity, campaign ROI, case resolution times, and customer profitability, enabling organizations to anticipate trends, identify emerging opportunities, and proactively adapt their strategies for future growth.

9) On-premise and Cloud Options

Organizations can choose between cloud or on-premise Dynamics CRM deployments based on preferences. The same functionality is available across platforms, providing flexibility. Hybrid environments also possible to balance cloud scalability with on-premise control.

With robust capabilities optimized for financial services, it's clear why industry leaders rely on Dynamics CRM as a trusted platform to enhance productivity, security, and customer experiences across the business.

As consumer expectations evolve, Dynamics CRM offers an adaptable solution to outpace the competition. What potential use cases could Dynamics CRM enable in your financial organization?
