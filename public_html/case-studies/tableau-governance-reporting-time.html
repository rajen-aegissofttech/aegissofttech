80% Reduced reporting Turnaround Time With a Governed Tableau Environment

Centralized Enterprise Analytics Platform | Single Version of Truth | Real-Time Monitoring

At a Glance

IndustryManufacturing & Supply Chain
ServicesEnterprise Analytics & Supply Chain Intelligence
ChallengeFragmented information across multiple systems, manual Excel reporting, isolated team metrics, unstandardized supplier measurement, and an unscalable reporting system.
SolutionA centralized enterprise analytics platform integrating SAP ERP, MES, QMS, and TMS data into a governed Tableau environment.
Key ResultCompletely eliminated manual daily reports, established a single version of truth, enabled real-time monitoring, and reduced reporting turnaround times by over 80%.

About the Client

The client is a global manufacturing and supply chain enterprise operating multiple manufacturing facilities across India and South-East Asia. They supply major industrial, and consumer electronics, and automotive manufacturers.

The Challenge

The client’s daily business operations relied on several disparate enterprise applications, including a Manufacturing Execution System (MES), SAP ERP, a Transport Management System (TMS), and a Quality Management System (QMS). Even though they were capturing massive amounts of transactional data, their business reporting remained highly fragmented and heavily dependent on spreadsheets.

They approached Aegis Softtech to eliminate this analytical and operational issue:

  • Siloed and Distributed Data: Business information was completely spread across separate enterprise applications.
  • Manual Blockages: Daily operational reports were being manually compiled and prepared using Excel spreadsheets. This was not scaling alongside their growing production volumes.
  • Isolated Reporting Systems: Procurement, logistics, quality, and production teams operated entirely within isolated reporting environments.
  • Inconsistent Metrics: Supplier performance measurements were very different across distinct manufacturing plants. Customer delivery performance required tedious manual reconciliation.
  • Blind Spots for Leadership: Corporate leadership was lacking visibility into overarching production efficiency.

The Solution

The engineering team designed and implemented a centralized analytics platform using Tableau to deliver near real-time operational insights within a governed environment.

Data Integration and Automatic Extraction

To bring multi-source data together, we build an integration architecture. It unified data across SAP ERP, QMS, TMS, and MES software. Python-based integration services were utilized to fully automate data extraction and transformation processes, removing manual handling.

Automated Data Preparation

Our team of Tableau certified developers developed comprehensive data preparation workflows using Tableau Prep Builder, reinforced by custom Python automation. These workflows structured data flows for:

  • Procurement & Supplier Performance
  • Production Performance
  • Quality Analytics
  • Inventory Management
  • Logistics & Delivery Performance

The accompanying Python automation directly supported foundational infrastructure needs, including REST API connections, SAP integration, incremental data loading, automated data validation rules, and SFTP file ingestion.

Governed Data Sources and Standardized KPIs

The team established certified Tableau Published Data Sources. This central repository imbibed standardized KPIs and calculations directly into the data layer. It created a single version of truth across manufacturing, procurement, quality, and logistics teams. Standardized indicators included:

  • Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
  • On-Time In-Full (OTIF)
  • Supplier Performance Index
  • First Pass Yield
  • Inventory Days of Cover
  • Production Order Variance

Advanced Dashboard Development

The team built highly interactive operational dashboards configured with drill-down analytics, Level of Detail (LOD) Expressions, dynamic parameters, interactive filters, Table Calculations, exception-based alerts, and geographic mapping. The functional modules delivered include:

  • Supply Chain Control Tower
  • Supplier Performance Dashboard
  • Production Efficiency Dashboard
  • Inventory Analytics
  • Logistics & Delivery Dashboard
  • Customer Delivery Performance

The Results

The enterprise analytics platform our team formed completely transformed the client's reporting workflows and operational metrics:

  • Over 80% Reduction in Turnaround Time:
    Reporting turnaround speeds were cut down by more than 80%, giving teams near real-time insights.
  • Eliminated Manual Reporting:
    The manual preparation of daily operational reports via Excel spreadsheets was entirely removed.
  • End-to-End Visibility:
    Leadership gained clear visibility across manufacturing, procurement, inventory, logistics, and quality pipelines.
  • Standardized Supplier Operations:
    Disparate supplier performance measurements were successfully unified into a single index across all operating plants.
  • Strengthened Compliance & Governance:
    The implementation reinforced enterprise data governance and secured customer delivery tracking and OTIF reporting.

What Made the Difference?

Strict Performance Optimization Tuning

The architecture utilized specialized Tableau Extracts, incremental data refreshes, data model optimization, and optimized SQL queries. This helped it maintain fast user interactions under massive enterprise data scales.

Context Filter Pipelines

During the implementation, we utilized strict context filters to organize internal processing structures. This ensured dashboard calculations and rendered scaled efficiently with data volumes.

Tableau Server Content Governance

The deployment was structured through Tableau Server with project-based content organization, automated extract refresh scheduling, active dashboard subscription management, and certified data sources.

Granular Access Control

The platform integrated strict role-based access management paired with Row-Level Security (RLS) to safeguard operational data layers across regional boundaries and facilities.
