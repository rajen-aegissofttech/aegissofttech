Faster Posting Speed | Zero Local IT Infrastructure Cost | Daily Lock-Up Resolution

About the Client

The client is a premier global wholesale distributor of commercial manufacturing equipment and industrial spare parts. It runs eight international distribution hubs and manages over 10,000 active SKU lines.

The Challenge

An outdated, on-premise Microsoft Dynamics NAV 2017 installation was severely constraining the client’s growth. Their physical servers began failing under the load of their scaling transactional data.

They approached Aegis Softtech to clean up complex legacy tables to restore speed and efficiency to daily workflows:

  • System-Wide Database Locking: High-volume inventory postings were regularly causing database tables to lock up. This was stopping sales desks and warehouse pickers in their tracks.
  • Extremely Slow Transaction Posting: The client’s processing batch sales invoices and multi-currency reconciliations was slow and frustrating, taking minutes per batch.
  • High Infrastructure Overhead: Maintaining physical SQL server clusters, managing local RAID arrays, and scheduling manual data backups drained IT budgets.
  • Lack of Real-Time Inventory Syncing: Their regional warehouse centers were suffering from synchronization delays. This was resulting in shipping errors and stock double-allocation.

The Solution

Our Microsoft Dynamics team mapped, cleansed, and migrated their complex distribution databases to a fast, clean cloud platform.

Legacy C/SIDE to Cloud Schema Refactoring

We handled the deep database restructuring to ensure optimal system performance:

  • Relational Database Cleaning: We analyzed and cleaned years of unindexed tables and temp logs from the client’s legacy NAV C/SIDE database, ensuring clean data transport.
  • Dataverse Compatibility Mapping: Our team remapped their complex custom tables and transaction histories to align perfectly with standard, high-speed Microsoft Dataverse objects.

Legacy Code Translation & App Extensions

We transformed outdated custom modifications into modular applications:

  • AL Code Modernization: Our experts rewrote their existing legacy NAV business rules as clean, modern AL language extensions. This ensured their compatibility with future automatic cloud updates.
  • Real-Time Warehouse Posting Hooks: Set up modern webhooks to instantly sync warehouse inventory changes across all eight global distribution centers.

The Results

The upgrade from Dynamics NAV 2017 to Business Central SaaS delivered outstanding operational speed and financial efficiency:

  • 42% Acceleration in Transaction Posting:
    The shift to cloud architecture reduced database locks and sped up invoice posting times by 42%.
  • 100% On-Premise Server Elimination:
    Migrating from physical on-premise servers eliminated the distributor's hardware maintenance. This saves thousands monthly.
  • Instant Multi-Warehouse Syncing:
    Their warehouse coordinators can now view live inventory levels across all eight locations instantly. It eliminates stock double-allocation.
  • Reduced Financial Close Time:
    Clean financial database tables and modern workflows cut end-of-month financial closing times by 35%.

What Made the Difference?

Isolated Parallel Sandbox Dry Runs

Our team built an isolated parallel cloud sandbox. We ran live transactional tests alongside their old NAV system for two weeks to verify every ledger and calculation before the actual cutover.

Multi-Entity Shadow Cutover Routing

We scheduled the data transfer in phases over low-traffic weekends. This structured rollout ensured their individual regional offices transitioned to the new Business Central system one by one, keeping their global network operational.

Progressive User Role Simplification

Our team customized the interface based on the specific job roles of their staff members. Customizing dashboards for accountants enabled teams to adapt quickly and reduce training time.
