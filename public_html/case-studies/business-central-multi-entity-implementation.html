Our Dynamics 365 CRM Implementation Eliminated 5 Fragmented Software Silos

Real-Time Reporting Accuracy | Reduced End-of-Month Financial Closing Times

At a Glance

IndustryCorporate Financial Services & Wealth Management
ServicesFull-Lifecycle Dynamics 365 Business Central Implementation, Multi-Entity Financial Architecture, Inventory Staging, Cross-Department Process Consolidation
ChallengeSevere operational blindness, fragmented supply chains, and duplicated efforts caused by running 5 disparate, disconnected legacy ERP legacy systems.
SolutionConsolidated all 5 legacy databases into a single, unified instance of Dynamics 365 Business Central across finance, warehousing, procurement, and operations.
Key ResultCompletely eliminated 5 fragmented software silos, unlocked 100% real-time reporting accuracy, and reduced end-of-month financial closing times by 60%.

About the Client

The client is a premier multi-facility industrial manufacturer. They specialize in precision automotive components and manage 3 distinct assembly production plants, 4 international legal corporate entities, and 2 regional distribution hubs.

The Challenge

Extreme software fragmentation was blocking the client’s operations. Over the years of regional expansion, different divisions had deployed 5 distinct, localized ERP tools that could not communicate with each other. This had led to serious operational blind spots.

They approached Aegis Softtech to deploy a comprehensive Microsoft Dynamics 365 BC implementation to permanently eliminate the 5 fragmented legacy systems and build a unified operational core:

  • Fragmented Supply Chain Visibility: Their procurement teams were not able to view real-time inventory levels across separate facilities. This was leading to a constant cycle of stockouts and over-purchasing.
  • Delayed Corporate Decision-Making: The client’s financial leaders had to manually export, scrub, and paste spreadsheets from 5 different databases.
  • Stalled Production Operations: Their inventory and finance data lines were unlinked. Consequently, order processing and raw material validation loops dragged on for days.
  • High Maintenance Overhead: Managing 5 independent software licenses, specialized server hardware, and maintenance terms was draining internal IT budgets.

The Solution

Our expert Microsoft Dynamics 365 team executed a structured rollout to unify all 4 corporate entities into a high-performance cloud ecosystem.

Complete ERP Consolidation & Data Staging

We systematically replaced the disjointed software infrastructure with a single, scalable cloud architecture:

  • Unified Financial Data Core: Our experts set up Dynamics 365 Business Central. This now serves as the single source of truth for global procurement, finance, shop-floor operations, and inventory management.
  • Structured Data Migration Pipeline: We managed the ELT of millions of data rows into a clean schema inside Microsoft Dataverse.

Automated Workflows & Real-Time Intelligence

Our implementation engineers streamlined their cross-departmental operations by embedding modern process automation directly into the system:

  • Intercompany Transaction Automations: Our team programmed background business logic routines that instantly record and balance transactions between internal legal entities with zero manual work.
  • Real-Time Power BI Dashboard Engine: We integrated native reporting tools directly with BC’s tables. This gives executives interactive dashboards as required, showing active inventory runs and consolidated profit margins.

The Results

Our Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation delivered exceptional operational velocity and clarity:

  • 100% Elimination of Software Silos:
    Successfully closed out all 5 legacy ERP tools. We moved the entire corporate footprint onto a single cloud platform.
  • 60% Faster Financial Closings:
    Automated intercompany balancing and shared data pools. This trimmed the monthly closing timelines from 15 business days to less than 6 days.
  • Real-Time Operational Insights:
    Their executives now have to access accurate dashboards on demand. This shows them stock availability, multi-entity profit data, and production costs in seconds.
  • Drastic Cost Optimization:
    Eliminating multiple legacy licenses and local server hosting fees cut annual IT software maintenance overhead by a decisive 35%.

What Made the Difference?

Single Accountable Delivery Team

We managed every stage of the rollout, from initial discovery audits to data mapping and post-go-live care, with no vendor handoffs.

Data Governance Early On

We established clear data cleansing and formatting standards before building the system. This ensured no broken records or duplicates entered the clean production environment.

Phased Go-Live Rollouts

We skipped a high-risk big bang deployment. Our architects instead rolled out the core modules to entities in structured, manageable phases to eliminate business risk.
