ServiceNow App Modernizes Patient Screening for Faster Clinical Decisions

Faster Clinical Decisions | Improved Data Accuracy | HIPAA-Aligned Screening Workflows

At a Glance

IndustryHealthcare
OperationsGlobal · Multi-Hospital Network
SolutionCustom Patient Screening Application
ServicesServiceNow App Engine Development, Predictive Screening, ML-Based Data Validation, Workflow Automation, Healthcare System Integration
ChallengeLegacy patient-support systems slowed screening, increased data-entry errors, and relied on workarounds to support HIPAA governance requirements.
SolutionBuilt a custom screening application on ServiceNow App Engine with ML-based validation, automated cross-functional onboarding, and secure, auditable data workflows.
Key ResultsFaster clinical decision-making, improved screening data accuracy, streamlined onboarding, and stronger HIPAA-aligned controls.

About the Client

The client is a global multi-hospital healthcare network running patient services and clinical operations across multiple facilities.

Patient screening sits at the center of its care workflows, but the tools supporting it made collecting, validating, and processing that information harder than it needed to be. The network wanted to fix that without bolting on yet another standalone tool — and whatever replaced it still had to meet HIPAA-aligned requirements around data governance, traceability, and access control.

The Challenge

Legacy patient-support systems created delays and unnecessary manual effort throughout the screening process.

  • Slow Screening Workflows: Outdated applications and manual steps delayed the movement of patient information between teams.
  • Data Quality Issues: Manual entry and inconsistent validation increased the risk of incomplete or inaccurate screening information.
  • Limited System Connectivity: Legacy workflows made it difficult to coordinate information and activities across departments.
  • Manual Onboarding: IT, HR, and Compliance teams relied on separate processes when onboarding users involved in patient-support operations.
  • Compliance Complexity: Existing systems depended on workarounds to meet healthcare data governance and audit requirements.
  • Limited Auditability: Tracking who accessed, reviewed, or changed sensitive information required greater visibility.

The healthcare network needed a secure application that could improve screening efficiency while embedding governance into the workflow itself.

The Solution

At Aegis Softtech, Our ServiceNow developers built a custom patient screening application on ServiceNow App Engine, combining workflow automation, machine-learning-based validation, and security controls within one platform.

Custom Screening App on ServiceNow App Engine

As part of the ServiceNow implementation, the application centralized patient screening activities and replaced fragmented legacy workflows with a structured digital process.

ServiceNow App Engine was used to create forms, business rules, workflow logic, role-based experiences, and screening processes tailored to the client's operational requirements.

Users could capture and process required information through standardized workflows rather than relying on disconnected systems and manual handoffs.

ML-Based Data Validation

Machine-learning-based validation was introduced to improve the quality of information entering screening workflows.

Validation mechanisms helped identify inconsistent, incomplete, or potentially incorrect data before it progressed further through the process.

This reduced dependence on manual checking and provided clinical teams with more reliable information for downstream decisions.

Automated Cross-Functional Onboarding

Onboarding workflows spanning IT, HR, and Compliance were automated through ServiceNow.

Instead of coordinating onboarding through emails and separate task lists, the platform could trigger required activities, route approvals, assign responsibilities, and track completion from one workflow.

This provided greater visibility into onboarding status while reducing manual coordination between departments.

HIPAA-Aligned Security and Governance

Security and governance requirements were incorporated into the application architecture rather than added after development.

Role-based access, controlled data flows, workflow permissions, and auditable activities helped establish a stronger foundation for handling sensitive healthcare information.

The approach supported HIPAA-aligned controls while providing better traceability across patient-support workflows.

The Results

The custom ServiceNow application gave the healthcare network a more structured and governed foundation for patient screening.

Faster Clinical Decision-Making

Centralized workflows and automated validation reduced unnecessary processing steps, allowing relevant screening information to reach clinical teams faster.

Improved Screening Data Accuracy

ML-based validation helped detect potential data-quality issues earlier in the workflow, improving the reliability of information used during patient screening.

Streamlined Employee Onboarding

Automating IT, HR, and Compliance activities reduced manual coordination and created a clearer onboarding workflow for teams working with patient-support systems.

Stronger HIPAA-Aligned Controls

Governance was embedded directly into application workflows through controlled access, structured data handling, and traceable activities rather than relying on separate compliance workarounds.

Better Audit Readiness

Centralized workflows provided greater visibility into how information moved through the application and how users interacted with sensitive processes, creating a stronger audit trail.
