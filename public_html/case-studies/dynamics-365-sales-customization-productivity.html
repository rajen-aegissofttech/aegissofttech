Automated Tracking Scripts | Single-Screen Interface Layouts | CRM Pipeline Visibility

About the Client

The client is a fast-growing regional logistics broker and commercial third-party supply chain provider. They manage enterprise freight contracts and corporate freight accounts.

The Challenge

The client’s sales workforce was spending a huge amount of time fighting their software rather than selling services. Their standard, out-of-the-box Microsoft Dynamics 365 sales interface was crowded with dozens of fields irrelevant to their brokers, which resulted in sales productivity hitting an all-time low.

They approached Aegis Softtech to deploy advanced system customizations to strip away data clutter, optimize sales rep output, and automate repetitive tracking tasks:

  • Stifled Sales Velocity: Reps spent nearly two hours every day manually logging emails, copying customer quotes, and booking follow-up tasks, keeping them off active sales calls.
  • Slow Deal Pipelines: A confusing lead-to-opportunity progression layout left sales reps unsure of immediate next steps. This was causing hot commercial leads to stagnate.
  • Severe Screen Clutter Resistance: The default layout displayed over 40 generic data entry boxes on a single page. This was resulting in massive interface confusion and widespread user pushback.
  • Blinded Sales Management: Poor system adoption resulted in incomplete forecasting reports. The sales leadership was unable to calculate monthly pipeline revenue with accuracy.

The Solution

Our expert Microsoft Dynamics 365 team re-engineered the CRM environment into a high-performance, automated sales tool.

Systematic Sales Layouts & Script Automation

Our developers used advanced C#/.NET plugin engineering and client-side JavaScript to customize the platform interface to match the fast-moving pace of sales reps:

  • Context-Driven View Optimization: Removed 65% of generic data boxes. This helped consolidate the entry form into a single-screen workspace, displaying only vital pricing and client data.
  • Automated Action Frameworks: Implemented real-time background scripts that automatically capture client emails and log phone calls. This eliminates manual documentation burdens.
  • Intelligent Lead Scoring Pathways: Built a custom business rule matrix that calculates and visualizes lead health inline. This guides reps toward high-value targets instantly.

One-Click Quote-to-Contract Bridges

Our integration specialists accelerated deal closure loops by creating smooth transaction links within the CRM layout:

  • Instant Proposal Generation: Created custom document templates allowing brokers to generate fully tailored corporate freight quotes with a single click.
  • Automated Escalation Triggers: Programmed instant notification loops to notify regional heads the moment high-value pipeline contracts require price overrides.

The Results

The rollout of our specialized CRM sales customizations delivered outstanding operational velocity and commercial clarity:

  • 45% Surge in Sales Productivity:
    Removing administrative data entry tasks boosted active sales call volumes by a decisive 45%.
  • 30-Day Reduction in Deal Cycles:
    Systematizing quotes and automating follow-up prompts reduced the average sales cycle by a full 30 days.
  • 100% Transparent Pipeline Reporting:
    Fast data workflows drove tool adoption to maximum compliance. This gives executives 100% reliable forecasting metrics.
  • Substantial Revenue Velocity Impact:
    The client’s sales teams successfully managed double the volume of open active opportunities without adding administrative staff.

What Made the Difference?

Tailored Workspace Engineering

Our developers rebuilt the form processing logic to ensure instant response from the platform during live customer conversations.

Elimination of Administrative Redundancy

Moving communication tracking completely to background automations allowed the client’s sales reps to manage relationships naturally without tedious data entry.

Focused Pipelines

Designing a clear visual progression tracker eliminated operational confusion. This ensures smooth deal movement through qualification phases.
