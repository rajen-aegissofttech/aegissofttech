75% More User Platform Adoption via Dedicated Dynamics 365 Support

Proactive Support Strategy | Targeted Micro-Training | Structured User Guidance Pathways

At a Glance

About the Client

The client is a leading multi-regional professional services firm. They deliver corporate legal advisory, B2B consultative services, and contract management.

The Challenge

The client started facing a sudden drop in employee engagement after the deployment of their new enterprise Microsoft Dynamics 365 platform. A structured, post-go-live post-implementation care was essential to ensure their day-to-day operations do not clog with software friction

They approached Aegis Softtech to deploy a structured managed support mechanism to ease user confusion, maximize enterprise system adoption, and eliminate ticket noise:

  • Critically Low Platform Adoption: Over 60% of the client’s account leads and consultants were actively avoiding using the new system. They preferred disconnected legacy alternatives.
  • A Flood of Repetitive IT Tickets: The internal helpdesk was completely overwhelmed by basic, repetitive inquiries regarding standard operational and navigation errors.
  • Wasted Enterprise Software ROI: High annual licensing investments were underutilized as users struggled to navigate basic management workflows and client billing.
  • Inconsistent CRM Data Entry: Reluctant usage led to missed data points and incomplete customer profiles. This was blinding the leadership to real sales and delivery performance.

The Solution

Our specialized Microsoft Dynamics 365 consultants engineered an optimization workflow to address the root causes of user friction.

Structured User Guidance & Adaptive Support Channels

We established a dedicated support network to turn the helpdesk into an accessible, educational user platform:

  • SLA-Backed Micro-Coaching: Our team built custom step-by-step navigation workflows and interactive guides within the system. This helped users solve common tasks independently.
  • Targeted L1 Functional Triage: Our helpdesk team actively coached users through difficult workflows during live support calls. This ensured a supportive and educational experience.

Proactive Usability Enhancements

Our support engineers ran proactive audits to optimize user interactions and fix the underlying frustrations driving user pushback:

  • Interface Cleanup & Layout Minimization: Hid unneeded data field arrays to match actual roles and daily tasks.
  • Process Automation Shortcuts: Automated repetitive administrative tasks, such as generating standard client service contract templates. This reduced the clicks needed to complete a workflow.
  • Continuous System Health Checks: Our team monitored usage patterns monthly to highlight screens causing users to abandon processes.

The Results

Our structured user-adoption support program completely changed the client’s software culture:

  • 75% Boost in Active User Adoption:
    Total platform engagement surged by 75%. It ensured the successful migration of the entire workforce onto the core system.
  • 55% Drop in Repetitive IT Tickets:
    There was a 55% drop in repetitive helpdesk ticket volumes. This freed up their internal IT teams to focus on core strategic tasks.
  • Absolute Financial Data Consistency:
    Systematic interfaces and confident usage drove compliance up to 100%. The management receives precise real-time performance insights.
  • Secured Enterprise Software ROI:
    The client fully capitalizes on its enterprise software investment by maximizing feature utilization.

What Made the Difference?

User-Centric Support Design

We customized the software interface around the client’s existing workflows, making the system more intuitive.

Preventative Bug and Friction Squashing

We analyzed ticket categories to modify confusing layouts and workflows. This permanently eliminated the issues driving repeat ticket submissions.

On-Demand Operational Help

Providing continuous micro-support ensured their team members felt fully supported as they transitioned away from legacy workarounds.
