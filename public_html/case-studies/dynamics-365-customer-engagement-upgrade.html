About the Client

The client is an established B2B services corporation providing enterprise technical consulting and software licensing solutions across North America. It manages an active pipeline of over 25,000 corporate prospects annually.

The Challenge

The client’s old on-premise installation of Microsoft Dynamics CRM 2016 was restricting its sales and business development units. Their legacy platform couldn't keep up with modern multichannel inbound marketing, stalling sales efforts.

They approached Aegis Softtech to update their data to Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement in the cloud, automate lead routing, and eliminate sales roadblocks to maximize marketing ROI:

  • Isolated Lead Repositories: Lead data gathered from webinars, partner portals, and websites, which remained locked in independent regional database tables. It required manual spreadsheet exports.
  • Delayed Prospect Contact: Sales representatives routinely took 48 to 72 hours to follow up with inbound inquiries because the old system relied on manual assignment lists.
  • Rigid On-Premise Workflows: The outdated 2016 workflow engine lacked flexible branching logic, which is essential to automatically move leads forward based on prospect engagement or company size.
  • Fragmented Customer Visibility: Sales executives lacked real-time visibility into pipeline stages. This was making revenue forecasting inaccurate and causing high lead-drop rates.

The Solution

Our Microsoft Dynamics team designed a migration and automated pipeline routing architecture to revitalize the client's sales engagement framework.

Legacy CRM 2016 to Cloud CE Update Execution

We migrated the outdated system architecture directly into a high-performance cloud environment:

  • Relational Schema Upgrades: Extracted historical lead, opportunity, and account records from the old SQL database, cleaning up unindexed fields for smooth integration into Microsoft Dataverse.
  • Unified Interface Deployment: We transitioned the business to the modern Dynamics 365 model-driven application interface. This minimizes clutter and gives their reps immediate access to customer touchpoints.

Pipeline Path Automation & API Integration

We turned a slow, manual qualification process into an active automation engine:

  • Automated Route Triggering: Our team built intelligent branching assignment rules that instantly match incoming leads with specialized account reps based on location, company size, and sector.
  • Omnichannel API Interactivity: Programmed cloud APIs to instantly pipe new leads from marketing engines straight into live CRM views. This removes data entry delays.

The Results

Our Dynamics 365 services helped the client upgrade to a modern, automated Dynamics 365 Cloud ecosystem, dramatically improving their sales metrics:

  • 50% Faster Lead Conversion Times:
    Automating assignments cuts the average time it takes to qualify and convert an inbound lead in half.
  • 4x Faster First-Contact Resolution:
    Automated notifications helped sales development reps connect with fresh leads in under two hours instead of two days.
  • 100% Elimination of Manual Data Entry:
    Real-time webhooks removed manual batch spreadsheet uploads. This saves hours of weekly operational overhead.
  • 38% Higher Pipeline Close Rates:
    Better lead prioritization and data-driven follow-up paths helped sales reps close 38% more deals across core business sectors.

What Made the Difference?

Cross-Channel Telemetry Mapping

We rebuilt their data tracking to capture intent signals, like page views or whitepaper downloads, directly within the lead record. This ensures their sales teams can see exactly what a prospect needs right from the very first contact.

Micro-Stage Pipeline Microservices

We split major sales milestones into automated micro-stages, ensuring large leads do not get stuck in vague pipeline steps. The upgraded system utilizes cloud background checks to automatically advance prospects as they complete actions, making pipeline movement smooth and reliable.

Role-Based Training Framework

Our team built practical sandbox workspaces designed for specific roles, such as Account Directors and Business Development Reps. Training staff with real-world scenarios before launch minimized disruption and ensured absolute comfort with the new cloud interface on day one.
