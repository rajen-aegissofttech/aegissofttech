Key ResultAchieved 88% active feature utilization across 1,100 employees within 45 days, reduced process bottlenecks by 45%, and cut onboarding cycles by 55%.

About the Client

The client is a leading healthcare equipment distributor and pharmaceutical logistics provider. It manages cold-chain deliveries across 24 regional fulfillment centers. We successfully upgraded their system to Dynamics 365. However, inspite this, their inventory planners and field technicians relied heavily on legacy spreadsheets due to unfamiliarity with the new interface.

The client choose our services to eliminate compliance risks and build team confidence in managing complex medical supply chains directly within Dynamics 365.

Training Architecture & Execution

Our Microsoft Dynamics team engineered an immersive learning curriculum to align platform capabilities with daily operational habits.

Persona-Led Micro-Learning & Scenario Workbooks

We broke down platform workflows into role-specific learning paths:

  • Task-Centric Workbooks: Our certified training experts developed modular guides for inventory supervisors, compliance officers, and medical delivery drivers. Each of these guides focus strictly on routine tasks like digital signatures and serial-number tracking.
  • Bite-Sized Interactive Simulations: We created small, interactive video walk-throughs accessible directly on mobile devices for warehouse staff and field engineers on the go.

Engaging Adoption Benchmarks & Real-Time Analytics

We transformed platform onboarding into a transparent team achievement:

  • In-App Skill Badging: We introduced milestone badges within the learning management system. This rewarded client teams as they mastered advanced capabilities like automated inventory replenishment.
  • Adoption Telemetry Dashboards: Our team built real-time Power BI reporting. It helped us track user login frequency, error rates, and form completion accuracy. With this, we provided targeted coaching for underperforming departments.

Key Drivers of Long-Term User Adoption

Embedded On-Screen Assist Prompts

We integrated contextual tooltips within live Dynamics 365 screens. Automatic pop-up prompts verify mandatory lot numbers as soon as warehouse personnel log temperature-sensitive medical supplies. This drastically reduces data omission errors.

Peer Support Network

We identified key regional shift supervisors and upskilled them into certified internal Dynamics 365 Mentors. This gave the team trusted colleagues on the factory floor and the confidence to immediately troubleshoot. It eliminated anxiety and accelerated comfort with the new system.

Workflow Streamlining Based on User Feedback

Prior to rollout, we restructured the mobile UI layout as per reports by field drivers. This simplified data collection and demonstrated that user feedback directly shapes the software experience.
