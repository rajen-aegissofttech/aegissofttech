30% Fewer P1/P2 Incidents | How Aegis Softtech Brought a Financial Services Client Back Into SLA Compliance

Proactive Problem Management | Instance Hardening | Faster Incident Resolution

At a Glance

IndustryGlobal Financial Services
ServicesProactive Problem Management, Incident Reduction, Instance Hardening, CMDB & IT Asset Record Management
ChallengeFrequent P1/P2 incidents, SLA non-compliance, and IT environment records that didn't hold up to audit scrutiny.
SolutionIntroduced proactive problem management and instance hardening, with disciplined upkeep of IT environment records.
Key ResultCut P1/P2 incidents by 30% and MTTR by 40% within six months.

About the Client

The client is a global financial services firm with an IT environment large enough that incident volume had outpaced the team's ability to stay ahead.

Client’s Requirement

The client came to Aegis Softtech with an IT environment where incidents were piling up faster than the team could resolve them. A handful of underlying issues kept surfacing across the engagement.

  • High Incident Volume: P1 and P2 incidents were landing often enough that the team spent most of its time reacting instead of preventing the next one.
  • SLA Non-Compliance: Resolution times were consistently missing the SLA targets the client had committed to, both internally and with regulators.
  • Reactive Problem Management: The team addressed incidents as they happened rather than investigating root causes, so the same categories of issues kept resurfacing.
  • Audit-Vulnerable IT Records: The IT environment's configuration records weren't accurate or current enough to hold up under financial audit requirements.

Solution We Delivered

We moved the client from reacting to incidents toward preventing them, while bringing the environment up to audit standard. Each part of the engagement addressed a specific gap identified in the requirement phase.

Proactive Problem Management

We introduced a structured problem management process through ServiceNow consulting that traced recurring incidents back to root cause instead of just closing tickets as they came in.

Faster Incident Resolution

With root causes addressed, the incidents that did occur resolved faster, since the team wasn't re-diagnosing the same issue from scratch each time.

Instance Hardening

Aegis Softtech hardened the client's environment to meet financial auditing requirements, closing the gaps that had left it exposed.

Accurate IT Environment Records

We established a disciplined process for keeping configuration and asset records current, so the environment's documentation actually matched what was running in it.

Ongoing Monitoring

The Aegis Softtech team tracked incident trends through the engagement, adjusting the problem management approach as new patterns showed up. Our ServiceNow managed services help maintain this ongoing monitoring and operational discipline.

The Results

The client saw the following outcomes:

  • P1/P2 Incidents: Dropped 30% within six months as proactive problem management addressed root causes before they turned into repeat incidents.
  • MTTR: Fell 40% as faster diagnosis and resolution cut the average time to fix an incident nearly in half.
  • SLA Compliance: Resolution times moved back within the targets the client had committed to.
  • Environment Readiness: Instance hardening brought the environment in line with what financial audits require.
  • Record Accuracy: Configuration and asset records now stay current, giving the client an accurate picture of its own environment at any point in time.

Technology Stack

  • Proactive Problem Management Framework

  • CMDB & IT Asset Management
  • Incident & SLA Monitoring Tools
