Accelerated Order-to-Production Cycling Speeds | Custom Business Logic Extensions

About the Client

The client is a premier multi-regional equipment manufacturer and heavy industrial supplier. It manages thousands of active material lines, complex multi-facility distribution channels, and custom engineering designs.

The Challenge

The client’s rigid, out-of-the-box system constraints were heavily restricting its operational backend. The standard platform configuration was not aligning with their specialized assembly approval loops, leading the departments into severe operational blockages.

They approached Aegis Softtech to rewrite system processing logic, establish a high-throughput automated workflow system, and remove manual intervention layers:

  • Overwhelming Manual Workloads: Their teams were manually inserting, verifying, and passing order specifications through three separate approval stages before manufacturing could begin.
  • Costly Transaction Entry Errors: They were manually copying data across unlinked product inventory lines and production sheets. This was causing incorrect parts allocations and expensive floor re-work.
  • Stalled Production Timelines: The administrative friction of moving paper approvals and manually reconciling bills of materials stretched order validation from days to weeks.
  • Fragmented Operational Clarity: Their client’s plant managers and procurement staff operated without real-time tracking. They were thus unable to predict production scheduling changes accurately.

The Solution

Our expert Microsoft Dynamics 365 engineers overhauled the client's system logic. We substituted their slow, human-driven data entry processes with high-performance, automated event code.

Custom Workflow Extensions & Transaction Hooks

Our programmers used advanced X++/AL plugin development to inject custom business logic directly into the ERP core engine. This automated the end-to-end fulfillment cycle:

  • Automated Data Validation Engines: Our team built transaction hooks that instantly audit and validate custom order parameters against component inventory limitations.
  • Instant Multi-Stage Approval Flows: Manual hand-offs were replaced with an automated electronic routing system. This enables instant signals and passes approved bills of materials to production lines.
  • Dynamic Part Allocation Routines: Configured custom table logic to automatically earmark and assign raw materials to specific active assembly files, bypassing manual clerical reviews.

Role-Tailored Custom Workspaces

We optimized employee focus and efficiency by tailoring the interface to match distinct operational roles perfectly:

  • Simplified Layout Deployments: Removed irrelevant input boxes and data arrays, leaving cleaner views that show only the specific fields required for a task.
  • Integrated Exception Dashboards: Interactive command views instantly flag ordering errors, allowing staff to correct data bugs in seconds.

The Results

Our specialized Dynamics 365 customization services delivered exceptional operational velocity and capital efficiencies:

  • 70% Reduction in Manual Workloads: Automating data approval and validation pipelines slashed manual administrative task volumes by over 70%.
  • 4x Faster Order Processing Speed: The timeline required to transition an order from initial booking down to the assembly floor dropped from 8 business days to less than 2 days.
  • 100% Elimination of Entry Discrepancies: Transitioning to automated transaction validation eradicated component mapping errors and manual data typos.
  • Unlocked Warehouse Resource Capacity: Eliminating repetitive paperwork freed administration the client’s teams to handle twice the volume of order requests without adding overhead.

What Made the Difference?

Deep Code-Level Customization

Our engineers built custom code extensions directly into the native ERP codebase. This ensured total system stability.

Fluid Event-Driven Automations

We structured the ordering pathways to move instantly rather than waiting for scheduled, end-of-day batch file updates.

End-to-End Tracking

Unifying sales validations directly with manufacturing floor files completely removed information silos between offices and factories.
