End-to-End Digital Testing Solutions

Digital testing services are a critical part of any digital project. They help you identify, analyze and solve issues related to the user experience of your website or app.

At Aegis, we provide comprehensive digital testing solutions to ensure that your website or software is user-friendly, easy to navigate and has no glitches. This helps in improving the user experience and increasing conversion rates.

We test websites and applications based on load time, accessibility and usability. We also check for possible bugs in the code or any other issues that might hamper the performance of your website. Here are a few of the digital software testing services we offer:

Test Automation

Automated testing is executing a predefined sequence of actions on a software application and evaluating the outcome against an agreed set of criteria. Through digital testing outsourcing automation, we reduce the time, effort and cost. We help you develop your test automation strategy, design, build and support it over its entire lifecycle.

Digital Testing Consulting

We help organizations understand how to leverage digital technologies like AI, ML, and Blockchain etc., to improve their business operations. We provide expert advice on testing these technologies, effectively leveraging our deep domain knowledge and seamlessly integrating them with your existing applications.

Functional Testing

Functional testing ensures that your website functions appropriately from a user perspective. Our digital software testing services identifies any issues with user experience or functionality before they become a problem for customers or employees. We conduct regression, smoke, system, and UAT as part of functional testing.

Non-Functional Testing

Non-functional testing (NFT) evaluates how well software performs after it has been installed and configured. We use NFT tests to identify defects that can affect a digital application's performance, scalability, reliability, usability and security and suggest ways to improve backend operations.

AI Testing

AI digital application testing uses artificial intelligence to test applications in real-time without human intervention or manual scripting. This method has become increasingly popular since it provides faster results than traditional methods. We use AI testing to detect bugs before they affect users through defect analysis.

Cloud Testing

Cloud testing is performed on various operating systems and browsers, which allows testing for compatibility across multiple platforms and devices. It also lets you scale up your testing infrastructure easily as needed, which can be particularly useful during peak season or when extensive features are being developed.

Our Workflow for Digital Application Testing

At Aegis Softtech, we believe that the success of your business depends on how well you understand your customers and how well you can reach them. That's why we offer digital testing services to help you identify and optimize the best user experience for your customers.

Research

We conduct primary and secondary research to understand your target audience, their needs and expectations, as well as their online behavior. This helps us identify opportunities for innovation, develop concepts and create prototypes that solve real customer problems.

Concept

Based on our research findings, our digital testing outsourcing experts develop ideas and concepts that engagingly solve your customers' problems. We do this through interactive application prototypes or simple wireframes based on the necessary testing scenarios, user base and application models.

Prototypes

We build interactive application prototypes that simulate how your product will look, feel and function when released on mobile or desktop devices. These prototypes allow you to test and refine the idea before committing resources to develop a functional product version.
