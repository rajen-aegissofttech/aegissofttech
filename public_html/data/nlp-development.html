The Fundamentals of NLP Services System

NLP can automate tasks such as information retrieval, dialogue management, question answering, and machine translation. The field is vast with several encompassing techniques for processing spoken and written language — parsing, tokenizing, and indexing texts.

At our NLP services company, we commonly use NLP to recognize and comprehend speech, generate text speech, and reason with uncertain information. Whatever the purpose of an NLP system is, it has four major components:

Analyzing and Modeling Text

NLP systems must understand how language works, what words are, and how those relate to each other. They do this by modeling language, creating structure out of the unstructured text.

Parsing the Text

Once the text has been broken down into words, the next step is parsing that text. Parsers generate a set of potential syntactic structures, which are word sequences that make sense.

Understanding the Text

If each of the syntactic structures is parsed correctly, it can be used to model sentences and parts of speech. And from this, the algorithm tries to make sense of what it is.

Recognizing and Comprehending

Once the system knows how to parse texts, it can recognize and comprehend them — that is, it can understand the meaning of what it's seeing.
