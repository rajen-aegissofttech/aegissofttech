Our Microsoft Fabric Consulting Services

Each service is available standalone or as part of an end-to-end engagement.

Microsoft Fabric Readiness Assessment

Prior to any Fabric recommendations, we perform an assessment of your current environment based on Azure investments, architecture maturity, Power BI use, governance preparedness, and volume of data. Provides a documented go/no-go analysis with justification.

Architecture & Azure Integration

We design Fabric as an extension of your existing Azure environment — not a replacement. Integrations include Azure Synapse Analytics, Azure Data Factory, Power BI, OneLake, and Azure OpenAI.

Microsoft Fabric Implementation

Structured delivery across six stages: fitment validation → architecture & design → platform configuration → phased data migration → validation & optimisation → handover & knowledge transfer. No stage is skipped; technical debt from shortcuts surfaces as performance and governance failures later.

Migration Consulting (Synapse → Fabric)

Workload-by-workload evaluation determines what migrates as-is, what needs rearchitecting, and what stays on Synapse. Phased execution with validation at each stage — not a single cutover.

Real-Time Intelligence & Streaming Analytics

IoT sensor data processing, financial transactions monitoring, operational dashboards, streaming pipeline triggers, and more, powered by the native Real-Time Intelligence capabilities of Fabric without the overhead of additional infrastructure.

Data Science & Machine Learning in Fabric

Fabric Notebooks, predictive model development on Lakehouse data, Azure Machine Learning integration for production deployment, and MLOps practices including versioning, retraining schedules, and model governance.

Governance & Cost Optimisation

RBAC, RLS, audit logging, and data classification are implemented as structural components. Capacity sizing aligned to actual workload demand, cost attribution mapped to business units, and alerting for capacity threshold breaches.

Ongoing Fabric Managed Services

Proactive performance monitoring, capacity optimisation, quarterly governance reviews, incident response, and regular reporting to data leadership — available as a monthly retainer for environments built by Aegis or any other team.

Why Consider Aegis Softtech as your Microsoft Fabric Consultant in India?

Choosing Aegis as your Microsoft Fabric consulting partner means working with a team that starts every engagement with "should you adopt Fabric", not "how fast can we start billing." Here's what sets us apart:

Fitment-based strategy

All projects start with a Fabric Readiness Assessment that evaluates your existing Azure investments, level of data architecture maturity, Power BI deployment patterns, and cost considerations. If Fabric isn't the right fit, we document that in writing with a clear rationale and a recommended alternative — before any build work begins.

Governance as architecture, not an afterthought.

Row-Level Security, RBAC, data ownership models, and audit readiness are design constraints we apply from the architecture phase. Adding governance post-live will be costly and disruptive. We plan for it right from the beginning.

Knowledge transfer

You run Fabric independently when the engagement ends. We document the platform thoroughly, train your team, and deliberately avoid designing anything that creates ongoing consultant dependency.

Synapse-to-Fabric migration experience

Different Synapse workloads migrate differently. We evaluate each individually — some move as-is, some need rearchitecting, and some should stay on Synapse for now. Every decision is documented with reasoning.

20+ years of global delivery

As Microsoft Fabric consultants in India with global reach, Aegis has delivered technology engagements across manufacturing, BFSI, healthcare, retail, and SaaS for over two decades. Our Fabric practice is built on that foundation — not assembled for the trend.

Time Zone Compatibility

Benefits of Microsoft Fabric Consulting Services

If your organisation runs multiple data tools, separate pipelines, reporting platforms, and governance systems, Microsoft Fabric consulting helps you consolidate them into a single, governed, scalable analytics platform.

Here's what enterprises consistently achieve with expert Microsoft Fabric consulting:

Saves on costs related to operational data by 25-40% through platform consolidation

Consolidating the processes involved in data engineering, warehousing, business intelligence, and governance to one cloud-based service. This helps avoid redundant licensing and infrastructure costs.

Cuts time-to-insight by 40–60%

by replacing fragmented pipelines with a unified lakehouse architecture. Data teams spend less time reconciling sources and more time generating business value.

Improves report accuracy and trust

Through unified semantic layer management. There will be no conflict in key performance indicator definitions or dashboard reports due to the fact that everything will depend on one certified data source.

Accelerates AI readiness

By creating the governed, clean data foundation that Azure OpenAI and machine learning initiatives require. Organisations with structured Fabric environments deploy AI use cases 2–3x faster than those migrating from ad hoc pipelines.

Mitigates governance risk

Through RBAC, Row-Level Security, audit logging, and data classification implemented from day one, and not retrofitted after go-live. Enables compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, and RBI data localisation requirements.

Accelerates migration by 30-50% faster

Compared to unstructured migration approaches. Per workload assessment of Synapse, Power BI, and legacy platforms, it prevents the rework and outages arriving from lift-and-shift migrations.

Enables self-sufficient teams

Every Aegis engagement includes complete documentation, training, and handover. Your team operates the platform independently after go-live, without ongoing consultant dependency.
