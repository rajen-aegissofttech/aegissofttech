Frequently Asked Questions About Power Platform

The Microsoft Power Platform is a suite of business intelligence, app development, and app connectivity software applications that enable organizations to develop advanced technology solutions. It is a Software as a Service (SaaS) solution that allows businesses to analyze, act, and automate data systems within their operations.

The Power Platform offers a range of tools and features that help to build custom apps, automate workflows, analyze data, and integrate applications.
