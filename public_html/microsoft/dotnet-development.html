.NET Engagement Models We Offer

Aegis being the top .NET development company in India offers quality services through flexible engagement models to enterprises of all sizes. Based on your business needs choose the best-fit model and save cost.

Hourly Pricing

You pay for each hour our .NET developers work. Good for projects with an unclear scope or changing requirements. Flexible, but costs can add up if the project takes longer than expected.

Dedicated Team With Fixed Pricing

You hire a team of ASP.NET developers for a fixed monthly rate. Ideal for long-term projects or ongoing development needs. Provides stability and predictable costs.

Time & Material Basis

Similar to hourly pricing, but includes costs for materials or resources used. Suitable for projects where both time and additional resources are needed. This model offers transparency in billing but requires careful tracking of expenses.
