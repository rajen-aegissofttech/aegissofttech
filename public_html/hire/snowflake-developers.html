Security, Compliance, and IP Protection

Regulated environments are where our security practices matter most.

  • MFA and access controls:
    MFA enforced on all Snowflake accounts, least-privilege permissions applied and reviewed weekly, and access deprovisioned within four business hours of engagement end.
  • HIPAA:
    Dynamic data masking on PHI fields, audit logging, and Business Associate Agreements signed before any healthcare data is touched.
  • PCI DSS:
    Column-level encryption, tokenisation via Snowflake, and separate virtual warehouses for cardholder data environments.
  • SOC 2 Type II readiness:
    Control mapping, access review cadence, and evidence collection pipelines.
  • Background verification:
    Criminal record checks, employment verification, and identity verification are completed on every engineer before day one.
  • IP assignment:
    All work product assigned to you. Engineers sign IP assignment documentation before any system access is granted.
  • NDA:
    Mutual NDA included in the standard MSA. Client-specific NDAs turned around within 24 hours.
  • GDPR:
    Right-to-erasure workflows, data residency enforcement in EU regions, and consent logging.
